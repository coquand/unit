| 6.2 Categories | The wild category record has independent object and arrow levels. For each arrow, the type of left- and right-inverse data is a proposition. Full subcategories on predicates inherit univalence. Types, pointed types, sets, families, groups, and G-sets have the corresponding categories. A preorder is univalent precisely when its object type is a set and its order is antisymmetric. Slices of univalent categories are univalent, and the universe has its wild slice. ## Correspondence with the Rocq statements

- Unit fixes the left functor as a module parameter in an adjunction. Thus the type of its right adjoints is a dependent sum of the right functor and the hom adjunction. Rocq packages both functors and includes an equality identifying the left one. The Unit fibre theorem fixes the representing objects and the hom equivalences together. [AdjointUniqueness](chapters/AdjointUniqueness.seg)
- The full-faithfulness and split-essential-surjectivity theorem includes both inverse laws for arbitrary precategories. The proof of the mere version uses univalence of the source to show that the fibre up to isomorphism is a proposition, also for a wild target. [EquivalenceCriteria](chapters/EquivalenceCriteria.seg), [WildFullyFaithful](chapters/WildFullyFaithful.seg)
- The 1-truncation exercise uses the equivalence between precategories and flagged categories. Unit identifies the paths between the images of two generators with the set truncation of their paths and transports the category operations across that equivalence. The resulting precategory has the specified set truncation as its actual hom type. [TruncatedPathGroupoid](chapters/TruncatedPathGroupoid.seg)
- For product cones Unit uses one equality between pairs of legs. The module `Book` proves the comparison in both directions with Rocq’s formulation using two path witnesses. [ProductCones](chapters/ProductCones.seg)
- The coherence conditions in the remark on wild precategories are recorded as types. Rocq does not prove a general pentagon filler for wild functors. Its Chapter 6 states the comparison of the two notions of group monomorphism and assigns the proof to Chapter 9. Its rational floor and ceiling example is informal. These are outside the Chapter 6 proof target.

The index tracks the mathematical results in the Rocq section summaries. Helper definitions and names can differ. The Unit check verifies the Unit terms independently of Rocq.

## Universe bounds

For a precategory with objects at o and arrows at h, the presheaves used by Yoneda live at o ∨ h⁺. Their natural transformations live at o ∨ h. Replacement gives a Rezk completion with objects and arrows at o ∨ h. Its universal property takes independent object and arrow levels for the target category. For a third independent level s, presentations of the input homs at s induce presentations of the completed homs at s. A presentation of the input objects at s also gives a presentation of the full type of merely representable presheaves at s. [RezkSmallness](chapters/RezkSmallness.seg)

These bounds are checked with unrelated level variables and without propositional resizing. [Category bounds](tests/CategoryBounds.seg), [small-presentation bounds](tests/UniverseBounds.seg)

Run `python3 check.py --universe-tests` from this directory. [Categories.seg](Categories.seg) checks the categorical development against the pinned category library. [All.seg](All.seg) also checks the results that use the book’s concrete groups, pointed maps and replacement-derived higher truncations. All Chapter 6 declarations are outside the optional coequalizer module.
