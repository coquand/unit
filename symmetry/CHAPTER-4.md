The circle is the existing K(ℤ,1). The ambient type of bicycles on sets in U u and the ambient classifier of n-element sets live in U u+. Replacement gives a bicycle's automorphism group and the alternating group classifying types in U u. The positive and negative [universe tests](tests/UniverseBounds.seg) check this distinction. Chapter 4 introduces no resizing or HIT postulate.

The Boolean value true denotes odd parity, so sign multiplication is XOR. Natural numbers represent cardinalities. The half-factorial theorem uses the division-free equation |Aₙ| + |Aₙ| = n!.
