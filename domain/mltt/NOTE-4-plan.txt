A. Step 4 is two developments

4a. Syntactic metatheory (NOT in the README plan; needed).  The Pi
clause of the relation records SYNTACTIC conversions
  Delta |- A0 = A0' : U,   Delta, A0 |- B0 = B0' : U
(Pi injectivity reads them off), and adequacy is relative to two
substitutions sigma, sigma' : Delta -> Gamma.  In the cases Pi, lam,
app the relation must produce Delta |- A[sigma] = A'[sigma'] : U, so we
need, as MIN Syntax/Substitution.agda (751 lines):
 - renaming of derivations (weakening), with lookup under renaming;
 - well-typed substitutions WtSub / WtConvSub (Delta |- sigma = sigma' :
   Gamma), lifting under a binder, extension by a term, identity;
 - the substitution lemma for typing and conversion, including the
   two-sided form (Gamma |- M : A, Delta |- sigma = sigma' gives
   Delta |- M[sigma] = M[sigma'] : A[sigma]); the rules have no
   lam-congruence, so the lam case goes by eta + beta;
 - presupposition: Gamma |- M = N : A gives Gamma |- M : A,
   Gamma |- N : A, and typing gives wf Gamma (MIN typing-ConvTm,
   typing-WfCtx); context conversion (eq-Pi has B' typed over A, the
   right side needs it over A');
 - syntactic equations: subst1 after renaming, subst1 of a weakening,
   traversal respects pointwise equality, identity traversal.  All are Id
   on Tm by induction on terms, over the kit (one proof per law, as Sem's
   SemTrav).
4b. The logical relation, its laws, adequacy, consequences.

B. Decisions for 4b, with the reasons found

B1. Head reduction: an indexed family HeadRed1 (beta; congruence in
    function position), HeadRed* its closure; deterministic; pp and uu
    do not reduce.  The relation uses UNTYPED head reduction; typed
    conversions appear only where the Pi clause records them.

B2. Recursion: on a fuel N (the paper's stage), NOT structurally on the
    witness: with U : U the term relation at the witness U calls the type
    relation at the ELEMENT witness, which is arbitrary (so no structural
    recursion on the type witness).

B3. Kripke over the fuel by construction:
       R (N+1) = new clause at N+1  AND  R N.
    Then R (N+1) => R N is a projection.  Plain fuel fails: in the lam
    case at N+1 the body needs the substitution related at N, and the
    Pi clause is contravariant, so downward closure is not provable.
    This replaces the paper's stability (Cor 2.33) and MIN's Stability /
    Levels (900 lines).  Adequacy is then proved for every N, by
    induction on N outside and on the derivation inside (the case N+1
    uses adequacy at N for the conjunct R N).  TO CHECK in NOTE-4: every
    law (PER, Conv, MonoTerm, MonoType, Join, head expansion) by
    induction on N with this definition.

B4. Clauses by upper bounds, no choice of a representative of the
    witness a (a is a meet expression; == classes):
       U clause:  Le a univ -> ...
       Pi clause: (A phi : Ty) -> Le a (pi A phi) -> ...
    om-like witnesses satisfy both vacuously (om-not-univ, om-not-pi),
    U and Pi exclude each other (univ-not-pi, pi-not-univ).  The value
    b(v) of the family is "every y with phi <= v -> y" (y : U), and
    app(u, w) is "every z with u <= w -> z": no application operation on
    types (the paper's app on C is not a step function anyway); by
    Lemma 2.8 the selections give such y, z.

B5. TyR (N+1) a T T' records T ~>* pp A0 B0, T' ~>* pp A0' B0', the two
    syntactic conversions, TyR N at the domain, and the family on related
    arguments (v : A, TmR N A v M M', Delta |- M = M' : A0).  TmR's Pi
    clause records ONLY the application part (related arguments give
    related applications at y, z), and its U clause T ~>* U and TyR (N+1)
    at the element witness.  Then TmR at an om-like element is Top by
    induction (MIN's Val2-Bot, no premise), which is what makes the
    identity substitution related over the bottom environment.

B6. Related substitutions over a FILTER environment rho fitting Gamma
    (Sound.Fits):  for each variable i and u in rho i, a in [[A_i]] rho,
    a : U, u : a:  TmR N a u (A_i[sigma]) (sigma i) (sigma' i); plus
    Delta |- sigma = sigma' : Gamma (4a).  Adequacy (Thm 2.35): for
    a in [[A]] rho with a : U and u in [[M]] rho with u : a.  [[ ]] is
    used only through Sem's lemmas and sem-*-eq (sem is opaque).

B7. Pi injectivity (2.37): adequacy at sigma = sigma' = id, the bottom
    environment (prin om at every variable; fits every well-formed
    context: needs typing-WfCtx and soundness), witness pi om om (in
    [[Pi A B]] by piF with a = om, b = om; of type U by t-pi).  Pi A B
    does not reduce, so the Pi clause gives Gamma |- A = A' : U and
    Gamma, A |- B = B' : U (identity traversal from 4a).
    Subject reduction (2.38): generation for app up to conversion, Pi
    injectivity, the substitution lemma (4a).
