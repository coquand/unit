1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE

Formalise the full equivalence of TYPES

  SC3_l  ≃  Triples_l

where, in ordinary mathematical indexing,

  SC3_l = Σ (X : U_l), Σ (x : X),
            (X is 1-connected) × (X is 3-truncated),

  Triples_l = Σ (A : Ab_l), Σ (B : Ab_l),
                Map_*(K(A,2), K(B,4)).

Use Unit's existing Ab and KK, not new group or EM-space implementations.
Unit's indices are shifted by two:

  X is 1-connected  = isConn three X,
  X is 3-truncated  = isTrunc five X.

Both SC3_l and Triples_l live in U_(l+1). The carrier types and EM spaces
live in U_l; Ab_l itself lives in U_(l+1). Write these levels explicitly.

The cocycle is an ACTUAL POINTED MAP. Do not replace it by its homotopy
class, take set truncations of the two sides, or quotient triples by
isomorphism. The target is not merely a classification of components.

Prefer to define the existing fibre realisation

  realise(A,B,k) = (Fib k, fpt k, conn3, trunc5)

and prove isEquiv realise. A chain of equivalences in the other direction
is also acceptable, provided its inverse is proved to agree with this
realisation as a map into SC3_l. Supply the equivalence, its inverse and
the two inverse laws (through the equivalence interface if appropriate).

No sphere axiom is needed or authorised as a dependency of this theorem.
Use only the existing Theory parameters: univalence, images, and
surjectivity of images. No new postulates, choice, resizing, HIT axioms,
or checker changes. Importing sphere-named files for parameter-free
lemmas does not mean assuming their sphere parameter telescopes; inspect
the actual declaration dependencies.

Out of scope: classification of arbitrary maps of triples, an equivalence
of homotopy categories, bicategorical structures, coherent 2-groups,
new sphere computations, and broad consolidation of existing code.

4. RECOMMENDED MATHEMATICAL ROUTE

Adapt the chain-of-equivalences proof of Theorem 41 at n=2, restricted to
simply connected 3-types. Do not begin by independently proving a general
mapping theorem between arbitrary fibres. A direct equivalence of data
types can supply the coherence without that detour.

The following is the proposed Unit specialisation, to be justified in
the informal note rather than treated as an already checked theorem.

A. Postnikov decomposition as an equivalence of data types.

For X in SC3, take V = ||X||_2. Its fibres E_v are 2-connected 3-types.
The corresponding family presentation has:

  V a pointed simply connected 2-type,
  E : V -> U_l, each E_v a 2-connected 3-type,
  a chosen point in E at the base point of V.

Relate these data to X by total space and the canonical truncation unit.
Prove an equivalence of the WHOLE data types, not just that each X
admits a family presentation. In the reverse direction, establish the
universal property identifying V with the 2-truncation of the total space.
Use Lemma 40 as the guide; avoid an unnecessary general n-group theorem.

B. Replace fibre families by coefficient families and cocycles.

The intermediate data over V have a family M : V -> Ab_l and a pointed
dependent section of v |-> K(M(v),4), pointed at its zero section.
Use the torsor classification and recognition of pointed 2-connected
3-types to obtain an equivalence with the family data in A.

The work is to show that the added group identifications and torsor
structures are recovered with contractible ambiguity. Existence of a
homogeneous structure alone is not a proof of this equivalence. Read
Hart–Milner's actual contraction argument and reproduce its precise
types. Keep base-point data throughout, rather than recover them merely
from connectedness at the end.

C. Trivialise the coefficient family coherently.

Prove or reuse that Ab_l is a 1-type, using the structure identity
principle for the EXISTING Ab record (identity corresponds to abelian
group isomorphism). For simply connected pointed V, evaluation at v0

  (V -> Ab_l) -> Ab_l

is an equivalence, with inverse the constant family. Use this equivalence
under the dependent sum INCLUDING the cocycle. The result has one group
B and a pointed map V -> K(B,4).

Do not choose trivialisations from mere existence; do not discard how
the cocycle's pointing transports. Ab_l is large relative to V: check
the universe instances of connected elimination/funext/univalence.
No resizing assumption may be added to conceal that issue.

D. Replace V by K(A,2).

Establish/reuse an equivalence between Ab_l and pointed simply connected
2-types, with forward map A |-> K(A,2). Recognition of each object is not
alone enough: show the necessary inverse/uniqueness statements. Transport
the full pointed mapping type along this equivalence under the dependent
sum. This yields Triples_l exactly.

E. Identify the realised space and assemble.

Trace the inverse chain to show it returns the pointed fibre of k.
Track the base point and path direction (Fib uses k(x)=base). Use the
existing fibre-path symmetry and torsor endpoint equivalences explicitly.
Once the inverse agrees with realise, transfer isEquiv to realise.
The final equivalence must quantify over all A and B, not just A=Z.

If a different route is materially shorter after reading the Agda proof,
explain it in the note and follow it. Embedding plus mere surjectivity
remains a valid alternative; no need to manufacture arbitrary choices.
