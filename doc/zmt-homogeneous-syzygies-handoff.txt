4. GATE A: CONNECT THE POLYNOMIAL REPRESENTATIONS

Prove the bridges actually needed:

  * a term-list polynomial evaluates to S, preserving zero, addition,
    multiplication and D-scalars;
  * equality by coefficients implies equality of the corresponding
    S-elements;
  * equal S-elements have equal four-variable coefficients;
  * every S-element merely has a finite term-list representative;
  * a finitely supported coefficient function with nonnegative exponents
    merely has a coefficient-equal term-list representative, and its
    S-image has those coefficients;
  * multiplying polynomial coefficient functions by g agrees with
    multiplication of their S-images, so syzygies transfer both ways.

but not mandatory if the maps, coefficient extensionality and truncated

6. GATE C: HOMOGENISE THE GENERATING FAMILY

For a vector h=(h_i), the shifted degree of a monomial X^alpha in its
i-th component is d_i+|alpha|. Define its homogeneous part of shifted
degree e by selecting those monomials. This decomposition uses only
decidable integer/natural degree comparisons.

For each of the finitely many h^k, enumerate the finitely many shifted
degrees appearing in its term lists (or use a proved finite bound).
Take all corresponding homogeneous component vectors h^{k,e}.
Keeping zero columns is allowed; avoid deciding whether an entire
coefficient vector vanishes. Attach its shift e to each column.

Prove:
  (a) each component is termwise HomShift d_i e;
  (b) h^k is the finite sum of its components by coefficients;
  (c) every component is a syzygy: since the g_i have degrees d_i,
      the degree-e component of sum_i g_i h_i^k is exactly
      sum_i g_i h_i^{k,e};
  (d) the component columns generate all ungraded syzygies, since
      they are syzygies and their span contains every old generator;
  (e) for a homogeneous syzygy y of shifted degree n, extract degree n
      from an expression y=sum_j c_j H_j. Replace c_j by its ordinary
      degree n-e_j part, obtaining homogeneous polynomial coefficients
      z_j of precisely the degree required by SyzGen.

Extraction (e) must work for integer n, including n-e_j negative. Such
polynomial components are zero, with finite support and degree witnesses;
do not exclude these degrees from the theorem.

Construct Identity and the exact existing homogeneous SyzGen from these
lemmas. No terminating full resolution or Hilbert syzygy theorem is
needed. Do not add a domain, field or regularity hypothesis on D.
