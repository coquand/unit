The proof below SIMPLIFIES the companion note: use a universal algebra
defined by just the residual monic equations. It is unnecessary to
descend all original defining equations, or to quotient the coefficient
base by coefficient identities. The auxiliary base can be a POLYNOMIAL
RING over Z. There is no need to identify a base-change with the original
algebra; a compatible map carrying the chosen generators is enough.

2. EXACT TARGET

Let R be an arbitrary commutative set-ring, with no CFN, discreteness,
domain, field or characteristic hypothesis. Let E be an R-algebra and
x_1,...,x_n a finite algebra-generating family. Let I be an ideal of R
such that E/IE is finite over R/I.

The conclusion is a truncated finite certificate:
  s in E, s=1+sum_j a_j r_j with a_j in I,
  monic polynomials over R annihilating s and every sx_i.

The central descent theorem may first take the following explicit
residual data, extracted from the finite-fibre assumption in a later
wrapper:
  a finite family a_1,...,a_t in I;
  monic univariate P_i(T) in R[T], of positive degree;
  polynomials H_ij in R[X_1,...,X_n];
  witnessed identities in E:
        P_i(x_i)=sum_j a_j H_ij(x).                    (R)

Its only nonconstructed theorem input is the corresponding CFN-base
ZMT certificate principle. Parameterise the principle by the finite
number n of algebra generators, so the result can be instantiated at
n<=3 after ZMT4 is done, and at arbitrary n after the generic proof.
Do not claim that the four-coordinate theorem already proves arbitrary n.

The principle may be restricted to the particular finitely presented
universal algebras constructed below. Give its exact signature and
explain how a future CFN finite-fibre ZMT theorem supplies it. Never
hide arbitrary-base descent itself inside that principle.

3. CERTIFICATE TRANSPORT

First prove a generic transport lemma. Suppose
  h:A -> R and phi:F -> E are ring maps,
  iota_F:A -> F and iota_E:R -> E are the algebra structures,
  phi o iota_F = iota_E o h,
  phi(y_i)=x_i and h(b_j)=a_j.

Transport a certificate
  s0=1+sum_j b_j v_j in F,
  monic Q0,Q_i over A with Q0(s0)=Q_i(s0 y_i)=0,
to the certificate over R obtained by mapping its coefficients and
elements. Prove polynomial evaluation naturality and preservation of
monicity. Carry the congruence witnesses and annihilating equations.
Neither h nor phi must be injective or surjective.

Only finite data are transported. Do not use a global section of any
polynomial quotient or image. Quotient representations and output
certificates may be given under truncation; eliminate them into the
truncated final goal.

4. THE UNIVERSAL COEFFICIENT BASE: NO COEFFICIENT QUOTIENT NEEDED

Collect a finite list of all coefficients of the P_i and H_ij, together
with the a_j. Repetitions in this coefficient list are harmless; preserve
the indices used by each expression. Form

  A0 = Z[C_1,...,C_m]

and the ring map h:A0 -> R sending each variable to its recorded value.
Use the existing integer initial map Z -> R and iterated polynomial
universal properties. Construct CFN for A0 from ZCFN and HilbertCFN
by iteration. The arbitrary target R need not be CFN.

Lift each a_j to its named coefficient variable b_j in A0. Lift P_i to
a MONIC P_i0 by keeping its leading coefficient literally 1 and lifting
the remaining coefficients. Lift H_ij to H_ij0 using the recorded
coefficient variables. Prove their images equal the original polynomials
by coefficient equality. No equality in R needs to be decided.

Build the iterated polynomial ring A0[X_1,...,X_n] and impose only
the finite equations

  f_i0=P_i0(X_i)-sum_j b_j H_ij0(X).                  (U)

Let F0 be its quotient by the ideal generated by these equations, and
let y_i be the coordinate images. Construct F0 as a commutative set-ring
and A0-algebra, using existing generated-ideal/quotient machinery.

The identities (R) show that X_i |-> x_i, C_k |-> their recorded values,
define a compatible map phi:F0 -> E. Prove quotient descent and
phi(y_i)=x_i. Do NOT assert F0 tensor R = E; no such assertion is needed.

Because the equations (U) are IMPOSED in F0, all their identities are
true there by construction. There is no need to impose the original
presentation identities on A0, no need to compute the kernel of h,
and no need to assume that the image of A0 in R is coherent.

5. FINITENESS OF THE UNIVERSAL SPECIAL FIBRE

Let I0=(b_1,...,b_t) in A0. Modulo I0F0, equation (U) becomes

  P_i0(y_i)=0.

Prove that F0/I0F0 is finite over A0/I0: the rectangular list of
monomials y_1^e1...y_n^en with 0<=ei<degree(P_i0) spans it. Monic
reduction variable by variable suffices; no Groebner basis, gcd domain,
field, domain, flatness or regular sequence is required. A finite
generating family is enough; freeness is not needed.

If the CFN certificate principle is formulated directly from residual
monic data, the same equations provide its input without constructing
that second quotient first. Nevertheless provide a bridge from the
actual finite-fibre CFN ZMT statement, so the conditional theorem can
be instantiated by the intended future result without changing its
mathematical assumptions.

Apply the CFN principle over A0 and transport its output along (h,phi).
This proves the arbitrary-base certificate theorem from (R).

IMPORTANT ABOUT THE IDEAL GENERATORS:
A CFN principle may return s0=1+sum_j b_j v_j directly, or only membership
in the extended ideal I0F0. If membership is used, extract its finite
generated-ideal witnesses under truncation; do not require a stronger
congruence datum from the future theorem without explaining the bridge.

6. EXTRACT RESIDUAL DATA FROM A FINITE FIBRE

Complete a wrapper connecting the usual assumptions to (R):
  * a finite generating family for the R/I-module E/IE supplies, by the
    existing determinant trick, a monic equation for each residual x_i;
  * lift the finitely many polynomial coefficients to R under truncation;
  * the vanishing of its value means P_i(x_i) belongs to IE, giving a
    finite sum of coefficients from I times elements of E;
  * because the x_i generate E as an R-algebra, represent the finitely
    many multiplier elements by polynomials in x under truncation;
  * concatenate the finitely many ideal coefficients to one family a_j.

State the finite algebra-generation input precisely: an evaluation map
from R[X_1,...,X_n] with every element of E merely having a polynomial
preimage is enough. There is no need for a finite presentation of the
kernel for this descent argument. If only the finitely presented case is
first implemented, say so, then discharge the extra kernel assumption
if it has not actually been used. Do not silently claim a stronger result.

The residual monic polynomials can be required to have positive degree:
if a monic degree-zero equation 1=0 occurs, multiply it by T and
transport the corresponding ideal-membership identity. Treat n=0 and
t=0 honestly. If the finite fibre is zero, the certificate need not
produce a nonzero s; 0 can belong to 1+IE when IE=E.

7. OPTIONAL STANDARD CONSEQUENCE, WITH CLEAR SCOPE

If the existing algebra/localisation APIs make it short, export the
standard consequence of the transported certificate:

  B=R[s,sx_1,...,sx_n] is finite over R,
  B[1/s] -> E[1/s] is an isomorphism.

Finiteness follows by reducing powers of finitely many integral
generators. Localisation follows from x_i=(sx_i)/s and the assumed
algebra-generation property. B is an image subalgebra; do not assume
it is free or that an auxiliary map is injective. This consequence may
remain a separately identified follow-up if its representation bridges
would obscure the descent milestone. The central required export is
the arbitrary-base MONIC CERTIFICATE from the explicit CFN principle.
