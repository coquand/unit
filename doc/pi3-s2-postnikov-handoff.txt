2. MATHEMATICAL ARGUMENT TO IMPLEMENT

Let C be the homotopy category of pointed simply connected 3-types.
Write [X,Y]_* for the SET of pointed homotopy classes, not the type of
pointed maps and not its propositional truncation. For T = ||S^2||_3,

  [T,X]_* = [S^2,X]_* = underlying-set(pi_2 X),    naturally in X in C.

Construct a pointed map kappa : K(Z,2) ->pt K(Z,4) such that, for every
abelian group B, the following canonical function is an equivalence:

  Phi_B : Hom_Ab(Z,B) -> [K(Z,2),K(B,4)]_*
  Phi_B(g) = [K(g,4) composed with kappa].                    (H4)

Also prove

  isContr [K(Z,2),K(B,3)]_*.                                 (H3)

Classically one takes kappa to represent u cup u; that description is not
required for the first implementation. An arbitrary abstract isomorphism
H^4(K(Z,2);B) ~= B is insufficient: establish (H4) for THIS kappa and the
coefficient-change maps K(g,4), with the necessary naturality.

Put P = Fib(kappa). Given X = Fib(k) for a target triple (A,B,k), a
morphism from the source triple (Z,Z,kappa) consists of

  f : Hom_Ab(Z,A),
  g : Hom_Ab(Z,B),
  H : K(g,4) composed with kappa = k composed with K(f,2),

where H is an equality of POINTED maps. Choosing f is choosing a=f(1).
For that f, (H4) supplies the unique g for which H merely exists.
(H3) removes the ambiguity in H at the level of homotopy classes.
With the correct mapping theorem this gives

  [P,X]_* ~= underlying-set(A) ~= underlying-set(pi_2 X),

naturally in X. Compare this to T's universal property. Yoneda, or its
explicit two-map proof, yields an isomorphism T ~= P in C. Apply the
pi_3 functor and compute pi_3(P)=Z from its defining fibre sequence.
Finally use pi_3(S^2) ~= pi_3(||S^2||_3) as GROUPS.

The universal object is free on one pi_2 element. It is not an initial
unmarked triple: the corresponding marked object is (P,1 in pi_2(P)).

5. THE POSTNIKOV MAPPING THEOREM

The full intended result includes the description of simply connected
3-types by triples and its extension to the homotopy category. However,
the sphere proof can begin with the restricted source (Z,Z,kappa).

Define Trip(A,B,k) and its realization Fib(k). Prove this realization is
simply connected and 3-truncated, and identify its pi_2 and pi_3 naturally
as abelian groups. Prove every object of C has the required Postnikov
presentation, with enough control to transport the restricted mapping theorem.
If presentations exist only under truncation, use independence/uniqueness
to descend the resulting statement; do not choose presentations globally.

Define a type of morphisms using homomorphisms f,g and pointed homotopies
H of the square, as in section 2. Build the induced map of fibres using
FibMap, with the necessary orientation of the square. Then prove the
comparison on SETS OF HOMOTOPY CLASSES, including compatibility with the
induced pi_2 map, postcomposition and identities.

Do not assume that merely commuting cohomology classes are all the data
of a morphism. There can be an H^3 ambiguity for other source groups.
For the source K(Z,2), prove precisely how (H3) removes this ambiguity.
In particular, H3=0 does NOT make the type of pointed homotopies a
proposition or contractible: it makes the relevant set of components
trivial. Justify translations between components of maps into K(B,4)
using the Eilenberg-MacLane additive structure or equivalent path lemmas.

An equivalence of raw mapping TYPES is not asserted by this argument.
The result required for Yoneda is an equivalence of hom-SETS.
Prove naturality in targets explicitly; an objectwise bijection is not
enough. A convenient normalization sends a map P -> X to the image of
1 under its induced pi_2 homomorphism.

7. FINAL ASSEMBLY AND GROUP STRUCTURE

Prove the equivalence [P,X]_* ~= pi_2(X) using (H4), (H3) and the
mapping theorem. For existence, a cohomology equality supplies a homotopy
only under truncation; eliminate it into the appropriate proposition or
use unique existence in a set. No global choice of homotopies is needed.

Compare the natural representing properties of P and T by Yoneda, or
construct inverse morphisms in the homotopy category explicitly. This
avoids building a larger categorical library than the proof needs.

Apply pi_3 as a functor on the homotopy category. This also avoids
extracting a representative pointed equivalence from a set-truncated
homotopy class: group homomorphisms form a set, so the functor descends.
Obtain an isomorphism of GROUPS, not just a bijection of underlying sets.

For pi_3(P)=Z, use the fibre sequence
  P -> K(Z,2) -> K(Z,4)
and its boundary homomorphism pi_4 K(Z,4) -> pi_3 P. The adjacent groups
pi_4 K(Z,2) and pi_3 K(Z,2) vanish. A proof through iterated looping of
fibres is also acceptable, provided it preserves the group operation.
Construct the inverse of the bijective boundary homomorphism by its
contractible fibres, without choice.

Final theorem: for SphereAxiom, under the original Theory assumptions,
an isomorphism of pi_3 S2 with the additive group Integers[l].intAb.
Adapt the existing group/abelian-group interfaces when writing its type.
No remaining parameters for classification, truncation or (H3)/(H4).
