3. STAGE 1: EXACT ABSTRACT STATEMENT

Inputs:
  * D, a commutative set-ring, with CFN K0 D in the existing sense;
  * a D-module Z, and a natural number v;
  * V = D^v, the existing finite free module;
  * a proposition-valued predicate B : Z -> Prop;
  * a proposition-valued relation Gamma : Z -> V -> Prop;
  * Gamma(0,0), closure under addition and D-scalars
    (negation can be derived or packaged explicitly);
  * zero-reflection: Gamma(z,0) -> B(z).

B need NOT have submodule structure as a parameter: the proof does not
use it. In Stage 2 it happens to be the predicate of being a coboundary
modulo J. Do not require Z itself to be finite, discrete or noetherian.
Do not require membership in Gamma or B to be decidable.

For b : Nat -> Z, suppose

  for every n, merely there is p : V with Gamma(b(n),p).

Prove, under truncation, that there are N and a finite family q_i in D
(0 <= i < N) such that

  B(b(N) - sum_{i<N} q_i b(i)).                         (RC)

Include N=0: the sum is empty. Prefer existing finite-list/family sum
interfaces. The proof must retain coefficients, not just double-negated
existence of a dependence. Indexing from zero here corresponds to the
order n+1 in the section-lifting application; it is not a degree-zero
approximation.

IMPORTANT: the p_n are only merely given. Do not construct or assume a
function Nat -> V choosing them all. Prove (RC) by free-list/bar induction
in V, retaining a finite prefix of chosen partners and Gamma certificates.
At the next index, eliminate ONE truncated existence into the
proposition-valued goal. Decide membership in the span of the current
free list using the existing finite-free (D). If dependent, extract the
coefficients; if independent, extend the free list and recurse. No
countable choice is needed. All witness types must live at declared
universe levels without resizing.

Useful source interfaces (read actual declarations):
  sag/artin-rees/{Comb,ModSpan,Spans,Coherent,CFN}.seg
  sag/artin-rees/{FreeDisc,FreeNoeth,CodedNoeth}.seg
  sag/artin-rees/SeqDependence.seg
  sag/noetherian/{Bar,Span,Sequence}.seg
  sag/acl-zmt/{TruncationCombinators,GradedDependence}.seg
  sag/acl-zmt/IdealPowers.seg (module and linear-map structures)

SeqDependence.Over/CFNFree treat an actually given sequence of vectors.
They are proof models, but do not already discharge the truncated-partner

It is fine first to prove the easier actual-partner theorem as a helper,

5. STAGE 2: ALGEBRAIC FOUR-CHART MODEL

Work over an arbitrary commutative set-ring D. Set

  S = D[X_0,X_1,X_2,X_3],  P=X_0^3,  Q=X_1^3.

Use finite Laurent polynomials with exponents in Z^4. Equality is equality
of coefficients; multiplying by P or Q is a shift, and their inverses on
the full Laurent chart are negative shifts. Retain finite-support
witnesses! Unrestricted functions Z^4 -> D alone do not represent actual
Laurent polynomials. Existing LaurentList/CechModules show how to impose
the finite-support condition propositionally.

General multiplication of two arbitrary Laurent elements is not needed
for this test: module operations and multiplication by the fixed
monomials P,Q and their shifts suffice. Avoid building a larger ring API
unless it actually simplifies the proof.

For nonempty E contained in {0,1,2,3}, let L_E(k) consist of homogeneous
finite Laurent polynomials of total degree k, whose negative exponents
can occur only at indices in E. Define

  C^q(k) = product over |E|=q+1 of L_E(k),    0 <= q <= 3.

There are 4,6,4,1 components in degrees 0,1,2,3. Fix an ordering on the
indices/subsets, and define the alternating differential by

  (delta c)_{i_0...i_{q+1}}
    = sum_{j=0}^{q+1} (-1)^j c_{i_0...omit i_j...i_{q+1}},

after restriction. In degree zero this is c_j-c_i. Prove delta delta=0,
D-linearity, support and finite-support preservation. Multiplication by
P,Q commutes with delta. Do not hide the signs in an informal convention.

Only FOUR coordinates are required. Avoid a premature generic all-r
framework. Existing files provide patterns, not a four-chart API:
  sag/acl-zmt/{Laurent3,LaurentList,Cech3,CechModules,CechOps}.seg
  sag/acl-zmt/{CechContraction,CechMonomials,CechH1Finite}.seg
  sag/stoneseq/Integers.seg (linked as ZInt in acl-zmt)

6. THE ONLY FREE-COCHAIN CONTRACTIONS NEEDED

Construct the following explicit finite-support-preserving operations:

  (a) pi on C^3(k), retaining monomials with all four exponents negative;
  (b) sigma on C^3(k), with delta sigma(w)=w-pi(w);
  (c) for closed c in C^2(-4), a in C^1(-4) with delta a=c;
  (d) for closed c in C^1(-1), h in C^0(-1) with delta h=c.

It is fine to prove a uniform contraction in all intermediate degrees
and all k if this shortens the implementation; the specialised results
above are the acceptance targets. Prove pi(delta u)=0 for u in C^2.
Selection of a monomial's first nonnegative coordinate gives the
contraction; use exponent/sign decisions, not decisions of coefficients
in D. Give a precise formula for the lower-degree contraction and verify
the signs. Pointwise identities are not a substitute for proving that
the outputs lie in the required finite-support chart modules.

7. TWENTY TOP COORDINATES

Let T20 be the finite list/type of quadruples a_i >= 1 with sum a_i=7.
Equivalently subtract 1 from each coordinate and enumerate quadruples
of nonnegative integers with sum 3. Prove this has length 20, with no
duplicates and exhaustive enumeration. It may be implemented as a list
with proved length or Fin 20; avoid unnecessary bijection infrastructure.

For w in C^3(-7), let top(w) be its 20 coefficients at exponents
(-a_0,-a_1,-a_2,-a_3). Prove:
  * top is D-linear;
  * pi(w) is the sum of these coefficients times their monomials;
  * top(w)=0 implies pi(w)=0.

The map induced by (-Q,P) on top cohomology would have size 2 by 20:
its first row selects -(1,4,1,1), its second selects (4,1,1,1).
This is an optional consistency calculation. DO NOT compute/generate its
kernel as a prerequisite. The two selected coordinates must vanish on
the particular certified top cycles, by the next construction.

8. EXPLICIT RAISING CERTIFICATE: NO GENERAL SYZYGY ALGORITHM

Take b in C^1(-1). A cocycle-modulo-(P,Q) witness consists of

  u,v in C^2(-4),    delta b = P u + Q v.               (E1)

Applying delta gives P delta u + Q delta v=0 on C^3, the fully inverted
chart. Define

  w = X_0^(-3) delta v in C^3(-7).

Since P is invertible on that chart, obtain

  delta u = -Q w,       delta v = P w.                 (E2)

Then p=top(w) is the finite partner of b. Verify all degrees, finite
support, and equalities. This formula supplies the top syzygy for this
example; do not ask a general syzygy theorem or regular-sequence theorem
to provide it. In particular, the intended resolution is

  S(-7) --(-Q,P)--> S(-4)^2 --(P,Q)--> S(-1)
          --> (S/(P,Q))(-1) --> 0,

but no general resolution category is necessary for this test.

9. EXACT STAGE 2 RELATION AND ZERO-VECTOR PROOF

For the Stage 1 interface take Z=C^1(-1), the module of ALL chart
1-cochains of that degree, not a quotient and not a finite module.
Define the proposition

  B(b) := merely h in C^0(-1), a_0,a_1 in C^1(-4)
          such that b = delta h + P a_0 + Q a_1.

Define Gamma(b,p) as the truncated existence of u,v,w satisfying
(E1), (E2), and top(w)=p. Prove its zero, addition and scalar closure.
The raising construction supplies a truncated partner for any b with
a truncated (E1) witness.

PROVE zero-reflection, rather than postulating it:

  Gamma(b,0) gives pi(w)=0. Put t=sigma(w) in C^2(-7), so delta t=w.
  Put u'=u+Q t and v'=v-P t. Then delta u'=delta v'=0.
  Contract them in degree -4 to a_0,a_1 in C^1(-4):
      delta a_0=u',   delta a_1=v'.
  Now b-P a_0-Q a_1 is a closed element of C^1(-1).
  Contract it to h in C^0(-1), giving B(b).

Check the two cancellations explicitly:
  delta(u+Qt)=-Qw+Qw=0;
  delta(v-Pt)= Pw-Pw=0;
  P u'+Q v'=P u+Q v.

These formulas are the relation-to-primitive theorem sought in Stage 2.
It is stronger to export the constructed primitive for an actual
certificate, in addition to the proposition-level implication.

  * the explicit top-coordinate map and its finite list proof;

The last theorem should say: if each b_n in C^1(-1) merely has u_n,v_n
with delta b_n=P u_n+Q v_n, then merely there are N, coefficients q_i,
and h,a_0,a_1 with

  b_N - sum_{i<N} q_i b_i = delta h + P a_0 + Q a_1.

No actual infinite sequence of chosen u_n,v_n,w_n is allowed. Do not
settle for a theorem taking Gamma's zero-reflection or the contractions
as unconstructed inputs. Such conditional helper modules are useful,
but the cubic instance must construct them.

Give at least one nonzero top-cycle example, not only zero cochains.
A useful choice is the top monomial

  w=X_0^(-1)X_1^(-1)X_2^(-1)X_3^(-4).

Its exponent tuple is (1,1,1,4), outside the two selected coordinates,
so pi(-Qw)=pi(Pw)=0. There are particularly simple explicit cochains:

  u_{023}=Qw,     other components of u zero;
  v_{123}=Pw,     other components of v zero;
  b_{23}=PQw,     other components of b zero.

Their exponents satisfy the respective chart support conditions.
With the fixed alternating convention, delta u=-Qw, delta v=Pw,
and delta b=Pu+Qv. The raising formula recovers this exact w.
This test therefore does not require a separate contraction in degree
-1 at cochain degree 2. Prove that the raising vector has the indicated
nonzero coordinate when instantiated
over an existing nontrivial CFN ring (or formulate the nonzero conclusion
with 1 != 0 explicitly). Do not introduce a new field axiom merely to
make this check. An existing integer CFN instance is sufficient.

The test certifies the finite-coordinate mechanism. It does NOT prove
full ZMT, a general finite-fibre lifting theorem, the Rees comparison,
or arbitrary-dimensional projective finiteness. State this boundary
clearly in README and the final report.
