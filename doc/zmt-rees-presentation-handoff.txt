1. MATHEMATICS

Data: A a CFN commutative set-ring; J = (P_1, .., P_s) in A[X_0..X_3],
each P_q X-homogeneous of degree e_q (ASSUMPTION, matching the graded
Cech setting; P_q given as term lists with HomDeg); M = A[X]/J;
I = (a_1, .., a_t), a_j in A.
Rees map  phi : R = A[U_1..U_t][X_0..X_3] -> M[T],  U_j |-> a_j T.

(a) Kernel = elimination ideal.  A[U,X,T]/(U - aT) = A[X,T], hence
A[U,X,T]/(J, U_1 - a_1 T, .., U_t - a_t T) = M[T] and
    ker phi = (J, U - aT) cap A[U,X]   (T-degree 0 part).
(b) Generators by eliminating ONE variable T: R is CFN (tower D_t of
zmt-weighted4, then four HilbertCFN steps, as in UngradedSyzygies4.Of.cfnS).
Over R, with the R[T]-span N of f = (P_1..P_s, U_1 - a_1 T, .., U_t - a_t T)
(T-degree bound n = 1), artin-rees/DegreeCor.Over..GenAt at m = 0 gives,
merely, a finite list g_1..g_L spanning N cap R[T]_{<=0} over R, each g_l
merely with a certificate in N:
    g_l = sum_q c_lq(T) P_q + sum_j d_lj(T) (U_j - a_j T).          (cert)
Read DegreeCor/Saturation to see the exact form of "in N" (XSpan NM,
sequences in T); turn it into explicit polynomial combinations.
(c) Two homogeneous degrees.
  U-degree: substitute U = aT in (cert): g_l(aT, X) = sum_q c_lq(aT,X,T) P_q.
  The T^n coefficient of g_l(aT,X) is ev_a(pr_n g_l), so
    ev_a(pr_n g_l) = sum_q [T^n] c_lq(aT,X,T) . P_q                (certU)
  directly, without a separate "the kernel is graded" lemma.  pr_n is the
  constructed coefficient projection of zmt-weighted4 (on the coefficients
  in D_t of R = D_t[X]).
  X-degree: with P_q of X-degree e_q, take X-degree parts (WeightedParts4.part,
  mulP-part) of (certU): the X-degree-d part satisfies
    ev_a([pr_n g_l]_d) = sum_q H_lnq . P_q,  H_lnq of X-degree d - e_q
  (zero when d < e_q).
  The bihomogeneous components [pr_n g_l]_d (finitely many: bounded
  degrees) lie in ker phi and generate it, since g_l is their finite sum.
(d) Why the degrees matter (for milestone 2): sum_l ev(g_l) a'_l
  = sum_q P_q (sum_l H_lq a'_l) needs H of the stated degrees to land in
  the right twisted cochain modules.
