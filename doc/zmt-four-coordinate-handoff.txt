3. EXACT POLYNOMIAL DATA

Let D be an arbitrary commutative set-ring and

  S = D[X_0,X_1,X_2,X_3].

Supply finite ranks a,b, nonnegative projective shifts
  d_i for i<a, and e_j for j<b,
homogeneous polynomials g_i of degree d_i, and a polynomial matrix H_ij
such that every nonzero monomial of H_ij has total degree e_j-d_i.
Formally the degree condition may be stated as
  d_i + degree(monomial) = e_j
for each potentially nonzero coefficient of that entry. Zero entries
are allowed even if e_j<d_i; do not impose e_j>=d_i on every pair.

These give maps
  A : F1 -> F0,    A(y)=sum_i g_i y_i,
  H : F2 -> F1,    (H z)_i=sum_j H_ij z_j,
where
  F0=S(-1), F1=sum_i S(-d_i-1), F2=sum_j S(-e_j-1).

Inputs must include:
  (i) the finite polynomial identities sum_i g_i H_ij=0 for every j;
  (ii) polynomial syzygy generation: for every finite polynomial vector
       y with sum_i g_i y_i=0, merely there is a finite polynomial
       vector z with H z=y.

An ungraded version of (ii) is sufficient if you prove the homogeneous
component extraction needed below. You may instead package a homogeneous
version with its precise quantification over all total degrees. Explain
which is exported, and do not silently assume a chosen kernel-solving
function when only truncated solutions are given.

This is a supplied partial homogeneous resolution, exact at F1;
no injectivity of H, next syzygy module, or full free resolution is needed.
The quotient S/(g_i) is only motivation: no quotient ring or sheaf
construction is required for the certificate theorem.

4. POLYNOMIAL ACTION AND SHIFTED COCHAINS

Reuse the four-chart differential with its checked alternating signs.
Build finite polynomial action on finite Laurent coefficient functions:
  (g f)(m)=sum_{c X^u in g} c f(m-u).
Prove coefficient-equality invariance, linearity, finite-support
preservation, degree and chart-support preservation, and compatibility
with polynomial sums/products needed for the matrix identity A H=0.
Coefficients of D need not have decidable equality. Exponent decisions
and finite polynomial lists are sufficient.

It is acceptable to use a finite-list polynomial model with equality
by coefficients, without identifying it with an iterated PolynomialRing
quotient in this milestone. If doing so, state all input syzygy hypotheses
in that same explicit model and document the missing ring bridge as
future work. Do not mix incompatible polynomial representations through
definitional equality.

For each q=0,1,2,3 define the D-modules
  E0^q = C^q(-1),
  E1^q = product_i C^q(-d_i-1),
  E2^q = product_j C^q(-e_j-1).

Construct A and H on these modules, commute them with the Cech
differential, and prove A H=0. Avoid identifying different module
carriers by repeated large unfolding: name the comparison maps and
degree-transport lemmas once.

IMPORTANT: the monomial contractions and pi are D-linear, not generally
S-linear. Do not commute them with g_i or H_ij. Only the Cech
differential commutes with polynomial multiplication.

5. TOP-CHART SYZYGY LIFTING: A REQUIRED BRIDGE

From the polynomial generation input (ii), prove that every
  y in E1^3 with A y=0
merely lifts to
  w in E2^3 with H w=y.

Here C^3 is the fully inverted chart. Give a finite algebraic proof:
  * choose one common monomial denominator X^L for the finitely many
    finite Laurent supports of the y_i;
  * X^L y is a vector of ordinary polynomials and is a polynomial
    syzygy of (g_i);
  * use (ii), under truncation, to lift it through H;
  * retain the homogeneous component of shifted total degree |L|-1;
    its j-th entry has degree |L|-e_j-1, and is zero if this is negative;
  * divide the resulting vector by X^L.

All support, degree and equality witnesses must be proved. If a finite
support list contains duplicates, deduplicate using decidable exponents
before reconstructing a polynomial from its coefficients, or otherwise
prove a reconstruction which does not overcount. Never assume that
arbitrary coefficient functions have finite polynomial representatives.
Eliminate the finite-support and syzygy truncations into the truncated
lifting conclusion. Do not choose an infinite family of representatives.

For a nonnegative integer e enumerate all quadruples of positive

7. THE GENERAL CERTIFICATE AND ITS PRIMITIVE

Take Z=E0^1, all chart 1-cochains of degree -1, and V=D^m.
Define proposition-valued predicates by truncation:

  Removable(c) := merely h in E0^0 and a in E1^1 with
                  c = delta h + A a.

  Gamma(c,p) := merely u in E1^2 and w in E2^3 with
                  delta c=A u,
                  delta u=H w,
                  top(w)=p.

Construct zero, addition and scalar closure. Do not require Gamma to
be decidable or Z finite/noetherian.

RAISING: from a truncated witness u with delta c=A u,
  A(delta u)=delta(Au)=delta delta c=0.
Use the top-chart lifting theorem of section 5 to obtain w, then top(w).
Thus each cocycle-modulo-(g_i) has a truncated Gamma partner.

ZERO-REFLECTION: from an actual Gamma(c,0) witness,
  top(w)=0 gives pi(w_j)=0;
  t_j=sigma(w_j) gives delta t=w;
  u'=u-Ht is closed, since delta u-H delta t=0;
  a_i=con2(u'_i) gives delta a=u';
  c'=c-Aa is closed, since delta c'=A(u-u')=A Ht=0;
  h=con1(c') gives c=delta h+Aa.
Export the constructed primitive for an actual witness, then the
truncated implication Gamma(c,0)->Removable(c).

No finite-matrix kernel computation, cohomology quotient, or generation
of the cohomology is needed. In particular, do not assume this
zero-reflection property or the primitive as an input to the general
certificate instance.

8. DEPENDENCE THEOREM: THE FINAL EXPORT

For D additionally CFN, instantiate the ALREADY CHECKED
CertifiedDependence with this Gamma. Prove:

If c : Nat -> E0^1 and every c_n merely has u_n in E1^2 with
delta c_n=A u_n, then merely there are N, q_0,...,q_(N-1) in D,
h in E0^0 and a in E1^1 such that

  c_N - sum_{i<N} q_i c_i = delta h + A a.

Include N=0. Do not construct Nat-indexed chosen u_n or w_n. The proof
should pass each truncated raising result directly to CertifiedDependence
and flatten the resulting primitive truncation, as CubicDependence does.

The only algebraic hypotheses of the general export are D a set-ring,
CFN for this final dependence step, the finite homogeneous polynomial
data, A H=0, and polynomial syzygy generation (ii). All polynomial action,
localised lifting, contractions, top coordinates and zero-reflection
must be constructed.
