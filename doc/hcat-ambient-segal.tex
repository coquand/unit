\textbf{Status.} Mathematical proposal, preceding implementation.
The target is the existing internal pentagon type,
\code{HcompPentagon.Pentagon}, for \code{hcomp} and
\code{hcomp-assoc}. No such proof is claimed here.
The checked development, including its explicit \code{hpent} parameter
and all homology constructions, is unchanged.
An independent internal refactor has subsequently been checked in
\code{unit/sag/hcat-segal}; see \code{hcat-segal-refactor.pdf}.
It derives the pentagon for new operations from finite internal
semi-Segal data and reuses the homology proofs. It does not establish
the ambient construction proposed here.

\section{The two coherence problems}

There are two distinct categorical structures. The ambient one has
objects $\Cat$ and arrows $\Map(A,B)$, namely functors. Inside $C:\Cat$,
objects are $\Map(1,C)$ and morphisms are fibres of
\[
 \Map(I,C)\longrightarrow \Map(1,C)\times\Map(1,C),
 \qquad m\longmapsto(m\circ I_0,m\circ I_1).
\]
An internal morphism therefore includes its endpoint paths. Equality of
its underlying map $I\to C$ alone does not establish equality in $\Hom_C$.

The current ambient parameters give composition, associativity and
unitors in \code{Wild}, the pentagon and triangle in \code{Coh2}, and
a specified $K_5$ equation in \code{Coh3}. These are ordinary identity
types; functor composition has no special conversion rules.
The internal composition and associator are constructed using the
geometric simplices $\Delta^n:\Cat$. Although
\[
 \Map(\Delta^n,C)\simeq\mathsf{Chains}_n(C)
\]
is already proved for every $n$, the compatibility of restrictions and
functor actions still uses ambient coherence. This family of equivalences
alone is not a constructed semisimplicial object with all matching data.

The present obstruction is concrete.
\code{HomTripleNormalize} expresses coherence of the existing
\code{Hom-map-of-comp} as two endpoint equations,
\code{LeftCoherence} and \code{RightCoherence}.
\code{PentagonReduction.assoc-on-spine} already identifies the chosen
associator on a 3-simplex with its defining pasting, and
\code{PentagonReduction.extend} reduces the desired pentagon to
4-simplex spines. These comparisons should be reused.

\section{A finite ambient presentation}

Fix the bound $5$ externally. Define a telescope of families
\[
 N_0:\mathcal U_{l+1},\qquad
 N_1(A,B):\mathcal U_l,\qquad
 N_r:M_r(N_{<r})\to\mathcal U_l\quad(2\leq r\leq5),
\]
with $N_0=\Cat$ and $N_1(A,B)=\Map(A,B)$.
For each of the four displayed values of $r$, $M_r$ is an explicitly
expanded dependent sum of proper faces of an $r$-simplex. A boundary
has one component $x_S$ for each nonempty proper subset
$S\subsetneq\{0,\ldots,r\}$, in increasing cardinality, with
\[
 x_S:N_{|S|-1}\bigl((x_T)_{\varnothing\ne T\subsetneq S}\bigr).
\]
This is notation for four finite expressions, not a claimed internal
recursive definition of all semisimplicial types. The total boundary
types may be large because their vertices lie in $\Cat$; the filler
families remain locally small.

Faces are projections of these tuples. Their shared subfaces are shared
variables. This use of the ordinary computation rules for dependent
pairs introduces no judgmental equation for composition in $\Cat$.
In particular, a nerve whose simplices are just chains, with inner faces
defined by composition, would not have this property: its face identities
are precisely part of the problem.

For $2\leq r\leq5$, let $\Lambda^r_1(N)$ be the same telescope with the
top face and the face opposite vertex $1$ omitted. For a horn $h$, require
\begin{equation}\label{eq:horn}
 \isContr\left(
   \sum_{u:N_{r-1}(\partial_1h)}N_r(h,u)\right).
\end{equation}
Here $\partial_1h$ is the already specified boundary of the missing face;
$(h,u)$ means its insertion in the full boundary in the prescribed order.
The requirement concerns the \emph{missing face together with the top
filler}. It does not assert that every full boundary has a filler, nor
that $N_r(b)$ is contractible for every boundary $b$.

\begin{proposal}\label{prop:proposal}
Present the ambient associative coherence by a 5-restricted semi-Segal
type as above, retaining the existing structural axioms for $\Cat$.
For the initial experiment, extend a normalized 4-restricted presentation
of the current \code{Coh2} structure, so that all existing choices are
fixed up to specified dependent comparisons. Supply the new coherence
by the degree-5 family and \eqref{eq:horn}, rather than by a separate
associahedral equation.
\end{proposal}

This changes the presentation of the intended external coherence schema;
it does not derive an extension from arbitrary \code{Coh2} data.
In particular, existence of the degree-5 extension is an input, not a
consequence of contractibility in degrees at most 4. Its equivalence
with the precise existing $K_5$ parameter is a theorem to establish,
not an assumption of this note.

\section{Normalization, units, and the dimension bound}

Normalization makes compatibility with the old operations a visible
part of the task. For example take
\[
 N_2(f_{01},f_{12},f_{02})
   :=\Id(f_{12}\circ f_{01},f_{02}).
\]
Its 2-horn fillers are a singleton, with centre
$(f_{12}\circ f_{01},\mathsf{refl})$.
For a tetrahedron write $q_{ijk}:f_{jk}\circ f_{ij}=f_{ik}$.
Use the current reassociator
$a(h,g,f):(h\circ g)\circ f=h\circ(g\circ f)$ and set $N_3$ to
the identity type between the two paths
\begin{align*}
 L&=\mathsf{ap}_{(-\circ f_{01})}(q_{123})\cdot q_{013},\\
 R&=a(f_{23},f_{12},f_{01})\cdot
       \mathsf{ap}_{(f_{23}\circ-)}(q_{012})\cdot q_{023}.
\end{align*}
Both run from $(f_{23}\circ f_{12})\circ f_{01}$ to $f_{03}$.
For a $\Lambda^3_1$ horn the unknown $q_{023}$ can be solved for;
cancellation of paths proves contractibility of its paired filler.
Thus specifying a tetrahedron is specifying an associativity comparison
with all its boundary witnesses, not replacing that comparison by
reflexivity.

The degree-4 normalization similarly encodes the supplied ambient
pentagon, including arbitrary triangle and tetrahedron boundaries.
The finite comparison with wild 2-semicategories supplies the method
for constructing this presentation. A comparison with the actual
\code{pentagonator}, including its orientation and path bracketing,
must accompany it. Keeping only agreement of binary composition is
insufficient.

Semisimplicial data alone have no units. For this experiment retain
\code{idMap}, the two unitors and \code{triangle2}. They can also be
represented by degeneracy data in dimensions 1, 2 and 3 on the
normalized presentation: identity edges, unit triangles, and degenerate
tetrahedra. The latter give unit coherence. We need neither a claim
that identities follow from the nonunital horn condition nor an
unbounded degeneracy structure. The existing \code{cat-univalence}
and internal \code{rezk} parameters remain in place. Completeness
should be compared with them only after unit compatibility is proved.

2 & binary composition & \code{comp}\\

The last row is the proposed new extraction lemma. Its dimension is
motivated by the actual endpoint calculations. For
$K:A\to B$, $G:B\to C$, $F:C\to D$ and $m:I\to A$, the underlying
arrow comparison involves four maps $(F,G,K,m)$, but evaluating an
endpoint involves five:
\[
 1\xrightarrow{I_\epsilon} I\xrightarrow{m}A
   \xrightarrow{K}B\xrightarrow{G}C\xrightarrow{F}D,
 \qquad \epsilon=0,1.
\]
This explains why an ambient degree-4 presentation should not simply be
declared sufficient for the current proof. Degree 5 is a proposed
sufficient bound for this route, not a proved minimal bound. Internal
degree 4 and ambient degree 5 refer to different simplices.

\section{The route to the specified internal pentagon}

The first test of the presentation should be a theorem producing
\code{HomTriple.Triple.Coherence} from the ambient degree-5 extension.
For the canonical morphism $(m,\mathsf{refl},\mathsf{refl})$, build the
two normalized endpoint pastings from restrictions of a common
ambient 5-simplex. Their common boundary and their identifications
with \code{LeftCoherence} and \code{RightCoherence} must be written
down. Then apply \code{from-endpoints}. Identity elimination extends
the result to arbitrary endpoint witnesses.

One can either extract the particular $K_5$ equality first and finish
the existing normalization argument, or compare the endpoint pastings
directly as dependent restrictions of horn fillers. The latter avoids
making $K_5$ a public axiom. In both cases a common contractible filler
space is useful only \emph{after} the specified pastings have been
lifted to it with their boundary paths. Contractibility cannot supply
these identifications for free.

The remaining proof obligations are the following.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Lift triple functor-action coherence to the triangle boundary
type. The vertex comparisons use ambient degree 4; the edge
comparisons use the preceding endpoint theorem. Retain all boundary
paths in this lift.
\item Prove that the current composition-preservation witnesses for
$FG$ agree with the composite of those for $G$ and $F$, with the
\code{Hom-map-of-comp} corrections. Compare the full 2-simplex
fillers, including their spine equalities, before projecting to
composition witnesses.
\item Prove preservation of the current \code{hcomp-assoc} by a
functor. Induct along \code{segal3}; use
\code{assoc-on-spine} to expose exactly \code{OnSpine.result}.
The preceding witness comparison supplies the needed compatibility
of its five factors.
\item For $T:\Map(\Delta^4,C)$, apply this preservation theorem to
the five associators in the internal pentagon. After the
composition-preservation corrections cancel, the two paths come
from parallel paths in a hom-type of $\Delta^4$. Those hom-types
are propositions, so their parallel paths agree. Apply
\code{PentagonReduction.extend} to obtain the pentagon on every
four-arrow chain.
\end{enumerate}
These are proof tasks, not new parameter fields. In particular, neither
the endpoint theorem nor preservation of associators should be assumed
in the definition of the proposed ambient structure.

An alternative packaging is to construct a 4-restricted internal
semi-Segal type $S_C$ whose total simplex types agree with
$\Map(\Delta^r,C)$, for $0\leq r\leq4$, compatibly with restrictions
and the existing spine maps. The already proved Segal equivalences
would then supply its filler contractibility. The word
``compatibly'' carries the work: independent levelwise equivalences
do not suffice to construct $S_C$.

\begin{proposition}[A sufficient internal interface]
Suppose such an $S_C$ has its edge family identified with the existing
$\Hom_C$, and its extracted binary composition and associator identified
with the existing ones as a dependent pair of operations and laws.
Then the pentagon extracted from $S_C$ transports to
\code{HcompPentagon.Pentagon} for the existing operations.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A 4-restricted semi-Segal structure supplies a pentagon for its extracted
composition and associator. Transport this term along the stated
dependent identification. Equivalently, eliminate the identification
first; the target is then exactly the extracted pentagon type.
\end{proof}
The requirement concerning the associator cannot be dropped: equality
of operations does not identify two chosen paths expressing their
associativity in an untruncated hom-type. This proposition isolates
a precise sufficient interface; constructing it from the ambient
degree-5 data is the proposed program above.

\section{Comparison with the literature}

Sattler--W\"arn explicitly mention a Reedy fibrant complete semi-Segal
presentation of the ambient wild category before adopting judgmental
associativity and unit laws in Axiom 1(5)--(6) of their HoTT/UF abstract
\cite{sw}. The present proposal follows the schematic alternative,
retaining their structural axioms and making a finite bound explicit.
W\"arn's local \emph{Higher-categorical headcanon}, Axiom 1 and Remark 2,
likewise treat infinite coherence as an external schema; Remark 32
emphasizes that constructed internal laws must retain their witnesses
to extend to higher laws. This is precisely why preservation of the
chosen associator matters here.

Capriotti--Kraus prove the equivalence between 4-restricted semi-Segal
types and wild 2-semicategories, before imposing truncation
\cite[Theorem 3.6]{ck}. Their later complete semi-Segal $n$-types
include an $(n-1)$-truncation condition on hom-types; we do not use that
condition for $\Map(A,B)$. Their finite dependent-family encoding and
low-dimensional unit comparison apply to the proposed normalization.
The degree-5 extraction and the comparison with this repository's
internal associator go beyond the stated degree-4 result. Completeness
is useful for univalence and recovering identities; it does not remove
the need for those comparisons.

Kraus--Sattler's work on space-valued diagrams provides finite diagram
constructions and a way of incorporating degeneracies \cite{ks}.
That is relevant if we later need a systematically constructed finite
simplicial presentation, beyond the unit fragment used here.
Annenkov--Capriotti--Kraus--Sattler use two-level type theory to express
externally indexed constructions internally \cite{twolevel}. Here the
bound is fixed before writing the telescope, so no outer equality,
UIP, or two-level extension is being used.
Sattler's marked semisimplicial \emph{sets} give a constructive semantic
model of higher categories \cite{sattler}; that is different from the
dependent-family presentation of the untruncated mapping types here.

\section{Integration boundary and next experiment}

Start in a separate checking root with the finite matching telescopes,
the degree-2/3 normalization, and the degree-4 comparison to the
existing \code{Coh2} data. Next add degree 5 and attempt the endpoint
theorem above. This tests the proposed bound at the current obstruction
before undertaking an internal nerve construction.

Do not change \code{All.seg}, \code{Composition}, \code{Associativity},
or \code{Homology.WithPentagon} during this experiment. Once a term of
the exact existing pentagon type has been checked, instantiate
\code{WithPentagon} with it. That preserves the existing groups,
structural connecting homomorphism, positive-degree exact sequence,
and sphere calculations. Degree-zero extension and categorical
naturality remain separate open homology tasks.

No new axioms have been installed by this note. The current
\code{Theory} parameters remain \code{univ}, \code{images}, and
\code{surjections}; all current Cat, copower, zero-object, suspension
and stability parameters, including \code{hpent}, remain unchanged.
Adopting the proposal will require an explicit finite ambient extension
parameter, together with proved comparisons. It must not be reported
as a consequence of the current level-2 parameters alone, nor as a
completed derivation of \code{hpent}.
