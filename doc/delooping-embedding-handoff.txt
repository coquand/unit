1. OBJECTIVE (the organising principle is David Wärn's delooping embedding)

  Let SC4 = (Y : U l) * (y : Y) * isConn four Y * isTrunc six Y   (pointed 2-connected 4-types)
  and SC3 = ClassPost.SC3 (pointed 1-connected 3-types).  Prove

    (E)  Omega : SC4 -> SC3,  (Y, y) |-> (Id Y y y, refl y, ..),  is an EMBEDDING,

  hence, with the classification,

    (C)  SC4 ~ (t : Triples) * Deloopable t,   Deloopable t a proposition
         (the fibre of the embedding over realise t).

  Then, inside the deloopable triples:
    1. (Z, Z/2, kbar) is deloopable: R = Fib kbar ~pt Omega BR (DeloopR.omega-BR; BR-conn,
       BR-trunc give BR : SC4).
    2. The marked universal property of R (equivalently of BR) among deloopable triples /
       their deloopings — THE SUBSTANTIVE STEP (see 3).
    3. Transfer through looping to identify BR with ||S3||_4 and pi4 S3 = pi3 R = Z/2,
       aiming to remove Presentation4.Pres4 from the new assembly.
  The existing Pi4Sphere proof stays as a checked reference; do not modify it.

2. (E) — proposed proof, from existing declarations (verify the exact signatures)

  a) Loops on maps.  For Y, Y' in SC4, loop : Map_*(Y, Y') -> Map_*(Omega Y, Omega Y') has
     contractible fibres: the fibre over f is DeloopMaps.Maps[Y,y,Y',y'].Over[f].Unloop, and
       Over[f].cor11 (c : Nat) (cA : isConn (suc (suc c)) A)
                     (tB : isTrunc (N.plus (suc c) (N.plus (suc c) zero)) B) : isContr Unloop
     at c = 2 needs exactly isConn 4 Y and isTrunc 6 Y' (Presentation.Rec.cell3 already calls
     cor11 two this way).  So loop is an equivalence of pointed-map types (Whitehead.contr-equiv).
  b) Equivalences.  loop F is an equivalence iff F is (Whitehead.Pointed[..].equiv: from el
     and connectedness; conversely LoopIso.loopPE).  So loop restricts to
       PtEquiv Y y Y' y'  ~  PtEquiv (Omega Y) refl (Omega Y') refl.
  c) Identity types.  ClassBase.PtProp gives (u = v) ~ (u ~pt v) on both SC4 and SC3; check
     that ap Omega corresponds to (b) under these (J on paths; at refl both are idPt —
     Puppe.loop-id). Then Omega is an embedding (ap Omega an equivalence).
     Alternative to avoid the compatibility check: show the fibres of Omega are
     propositions directly from (a)+(b) via the contractible-total-space form of PtProp.
  d) (C): Deloopable t := fib Omega (realise t); a proposition by (E); compose with
     classification (sigma over the base equivalence; see PostnikovClassification.base for
     the pattern with SphereAxiom[l+].sigma-base).

  Write the informal proof first (new note, e.g. doc/delooping-embedding.tex), get an
  independent review, then formalise in small modules with an .opens spec.
