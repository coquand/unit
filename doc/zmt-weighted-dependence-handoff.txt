1. OBJECTIVE AND THE SHORTCUT

Formalise weighted dependence for the checked four-coordinate Cech
development by taking an additional homogeneous component of its FINAL
identity. Reuse CoherentDependence4 unchanged. Do not construct syzygies
homogeneous in a second grading, homogeneous top-coordinate partners, a
new graded noetherian induction, or a new relation-certificate theorem.
Those stronger constructions are not needed for this milestone.

Let A be a CFN commutative set-ring, t a finite number of coefficient
variables, and D=A[U_1,...,U_t]. The projective variables remain the FOUR
variables X_0,...,X_3 of the existing development. They have coefficient
degree zero; every U_j has coefficient degree one.

The g_j are homogeneous in the projective variables with degrees d_j,
and homogeneous in coefficient degree with degrees f_j. The inputs c_n
have projective degree -1 and coefficient degree n+1. The checked theorem
already gives, merely,

  c_N - sum_{i<N} q_i c_i = delta h + sum_j g_j a_j.       (U)

Project (U) to coefficient degree N+1. Then

  c_N - sum_{i<N} [q_i]_(N-i) c_i
    = delta [h]_(N+1) + sum_j g_j [a_j]_(N+1-f_j).        (W)

The q_i, h and a_j produced by (U) need NOT be homogeneous beforehand.
In particular no second grading on their internally constructed syzygies
is needed. This is the central simplification to preserve.

4. GATE A: COEFFICIENT RING AND PROJECTIONS

Build D=A[U_1,...,U_t] for arbitrary finite t, using iteration of the
existing PolynomialRing/PolyCoef/HilbertCFN interfaces. Include t=0.
Fix and document the variable order. Obtain CFN K0 D from CFN K0 A by
iteration. Do not assume the coefficient grading as an extra axiom in
the final concrete theorem.

Implement finite coefficient polynomials with exponent vectors of length
t, or an equivalent recursive representation over the iterated ring.
There is no requirement to build arbitrary-dimensional PROJECTIVE Cech
complexes. Only the coefficient polynomial ring varies with t.

For every integer k define pr_k : D -> D, retaining precisely the
U-monomials of total degree k, and zero for k<0. Prove:
  * additive and A-linear, zero, negation and finite-sum laws;
  * pr_k(x) is homogeneous of coefficient degree k;
  * if x is homogeneous of degree k, pr_k(x)=x;
  * a homogeneous degree-e element has zero degree-k part for k!=e;
  * if y is homogeneous of coefficient degree e, then
        pr_k(x y) = pr_(k-e)(x) y;
    likewise when the homogeneous factor is first;
  * a coefficient-homogeneous degree-k element merely has a finite
    polynomial expression using only monomials of degree k.

The homogeneity predicates must allow the zero element in every degree.
For negative k such an element is necessarily zero. Only exponent and
degree comparisons are decided, never equality of coefficients of A.
Do not use coefficient-domain, field, or nontriviality assumptions not
already present in CFN. A ring-equivalence package is optional; sufficient
maps, extensionality and truncated representatives are acceptable.

Intermediate generic results parameterised by concrete grading laws may
help, but Gate A must instantiate them for the actual iterated ring D.

5. GATE B: APPLY COEFFICIENT PROJECTION TO THE EXISTING CHARTS

The Cech complex is the checked four-chart complex over D. For a chart
coefficient function f : Z^4 -> D, define
  (Pr_k f)(m) = pr_k(f(m)).
Extend componentwise to cochains and finite shifted families. Prove that
it preserves their finite-support and PROJECTIVE-degree/chart-support
conditions: the old support list suffices since pr_k(0)=0. Prove
additivity, A-linearity, finite-sum laws and
  Pr_k(delta f) = delta(Pr_k f).
Do not claim D-linearity: pr_k is generally not D-linear.

Define coefficient homogeneity of cochains by the homogeneity of every
D-valued coefficient. This is independent of projective degree -1 or
-1-d_j. In particular the input c_n is homogeneous in coefficient degree
n+1 while all its projective monomials have degree -1.

For a term-list g in the PROJECTIVE variables, define coefficient
homogeneity f by requiring every D-coefficient of every term to be
homogeneous of U-degree f (zero coefficients allowed). Combined with
the existing HomDeg d g, this is a concrete bihomogeneous input.

Prove the action formula, for arbitrary integer k and arbitrary chart
cochain a of the proper projective twist:
  Pr_k(g a) = g Pr_(k-f)(a)
when g has coefficient degree f. This follows termwise from the
coefficient multiplication formula, not by commuting a Cech contraction
with multiplication. No compatibility of sigma/con1/con2 with the
coefficient grading is needed: project the ALREADY PRODUCED primitive.

Also prove, when c has coefficient degree e,
  Pr_k(q c) = pr_(k-e)(q) c.
Here q is a scalar in D, not a polynomial in the projective variables.
Keep the two actions distinct in the interfaces and proofs.

6. GATE C: THE EXACT WEIGHTED THEOREM

Inputs:
  A a CFN set-ring and the actual D=A[U_1,...,U_t];
  a : Nat, projective degrees d_j and coefficient degrees f_j;
  original term lists g_j over D, with HomDeg d_j and coefficient
    homogeneity f_j;
  c : Nat -> C_D^1(-1), each c_n homogeneous in coefficient degree n+1;
  lift : for every n, merely u_{n,j} in C_D^2(-1-d_j) with
             delta c_n = sum_j g_j u_{n,j}.

The given u_{n,j} need not have prescribed coefficient degree. No chosen
infinite sequence of them is allowed.

Apply the checked CoherentDependence4 over D. Under its result truncation,
write N=len qs and project its exact identity (U) to degree N+1.
Return, under truncation, coefficients r_i=pr_(N-i)(q_i), a primitive
v=Pr_(N+1)(h), and a'_j=Pr_(N+1-f_j)(a_j) such that

  c_N - sum_{i<N} r_i c_i = delta v + sum_j g_j a'_j,
  r_i homogeneous of coefficient degree N-i,
  v homogeneous of coefficient degree N+1,
  a'_j homogeneous of coefficient degree N+1-f_j.

Each a'_j keeps projective twist -1-d_j; v keeps projective twist -1.
Handle N+1-f_j negative by the zero projection. For all i<N, N-i>=1.
Include N=0 and the empty coefficient list; the primitive still has
coefficient degree 1.

The existing finite-sum convention uses qs=[q_(N-1),...,q_0]. At the
entry whose tail has length i, replace q_i by pr_(N-i)(q_i), using the
FIXED outer length N. Prove length preservation and the sum/projection
identity. Do not accidentally use the changing tail length as N.

The final theorem must not assume a weighted dependence result, graded
syzygies, pure top-coordinate partners, or the required small primitive.
All these conclusions must follow from projection of the checked (U).

7. GATE D: EVALUATION AND FINITE IDEAL POWERS

For a finite family a_1,...,a_t in A, construct evaluation
  ev_a : D -> A,   U_j |-> a_j,
using the iterated polynomial universal properties, and its coefficient
action on cochains. Let I=(a_1,...,a_t).

For k>=0, prove that evaluation of a coefficient-homogeneous element
of degree k is in I^k, WITH finite witnesses. The intended witness is
the evaluated finite sum of degree-k monomials. Use/generate a finite-list
ideal-power definition whose recursive form is
  I^0 M = M,
  x in I^(k+1) M iff merely x=sum_{j=1}^t a_j x_j with x_j in I^k M.
Or use equivalent finite monomial combinations with proved conversion.
Include t=0, k=0 and the zero element cases; no representation may
require a nonempty generator list. Do not generalise Artin-Rees itself.

For cochains, coefficientwise I^k membership is not the whole statement:
prove that evaluation of a coefficient-degree-k FINITELY SUPPORTED
cochain lies in I^k of the actual chart-cochain module. Reconstruct a
finite sum of A-cochains times degree-k monomials, retaining their chart
and projective-degree conditions. Finite choices under truncation suffice.

Evaluate the weighted identity. In particular return the witnesses
  ev_a(r_i) in I^(N-i),
  ev_a(v) in I^(N+1) C_A^0(-1),
and the evaluated equality. The terms ev_a(g_j) ev_a(a'_j) remain in
the evaluated equation ideal. Do not silently delete them.

In the future Rees application those terms vanish by its presentation
map; that vanishing/comparison is OUTSIDE this milestone. This task
exports an honest equality modulo the evaluated g_j together with
the ideal-power witnesses, not full section lifting or ZMT.
