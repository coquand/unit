3. PRECISE GENERAL THEOREM TO VALIDATE AND PROVE

Use the EXISTING Triples, Ab, Hom, KK, KMap, PtMap, Fib and realisation.
Write composition in diagrammatic order: f ; g means g after f.

For t = (A,B,k) and t' = (A',B',k'), define the intrinsic morphism TYPE

  Mor(t,t') = (a : Hom A A') * (b : Hom B B') *
    Id (PtMap (K(A,2)) (K(B',4)))
       (K(a,2) ; k') (k ; K(b,4)).

Base points are suppressed here only for readability. The last field H is
a PATH OF POINTED MAPS, including the coherence at the base point. It is
not mere existence, not equality of cohomology classes, and not just an
unpointed homotopy. Use named record fields/accessors where appropriate.

Construct the canonical map

  realise-map : Mor(t,t') -> PtMap (realise t) (realise t')

using FibMap.FibMap with f=k, f'=k', u=K(a,2), v=K(b,4), sq=H.
Prove it is an EQUIVALENCE OF TYPES, with inverse and both inverse laws.
The target means pointed maps between the carriers with their recorded
points, not a map between the structures SC3 themselves.

First validate this precise formula in an informal proof. The formula is
the intended theorem, not an additional axiom. If it needs correction,
give a precise reason/counterexample and repair it before coding.

This must classify arbitrary coefficient homomorphisms and arbitrary k,
k'. Do not assume a or b invertible, either source coefficient group Z,
or any special H3/H4 vanishing hypothesis. No sphere axiom enters here.

Important scope checks:
  - Object equivalence only identifies paths/equivalences; it does not
    automatically identify arbitrary maps with the displayed Mor.
  - Defining Mor as PtMap of the realisations makes the result tautological
    and is NOT an acceptable replacement for the intrinsic formula.
  - Mor and the pointed mapping types should be 1-types in this range,
    not generally sets. Check the bounds using cor5-map.
  - Homotopy H is essential data. For t=(A,0,0), t'=(0,C,0), the theorem
    must recover Map_*(K(A,2),K(C,3)); the two coefficient maps alone
    carry none of this information. Treat this as a mathematical sanity
    check, not a demand to build a separate trivial-group library first.
  - Objects live in U(l+1); for fixed small groups the displayed morphism
    types live in U l. Record all levels, including any conn-lift needed.

A possible proof route (TO BE PROVED, not a completed informal argument):
  a) A map F : Fib k ->* Fib k' induces a map of 2-truncations. Since the
     projections have 2-connected fibres, the base factorisation should
     be unique as the appropriate coherent data. Recover a by H2.
  b) Its maps of the 3-type fibres induce a homomorphism b : B -> B'.
     Show how these fibrewise homomorphisms form one constant coefficient
     map over the simply connected base. Do not choose points in all
     fibres, representatives of classes, or arbitrary identifications.
  c) Prove the compatibility of the k-invariants and recover the actual
     pointed homotopy H. A natural route is a theorem about maps between
     the families/torsors used by ClassFib. Fibrewise maps have a linear
     part b and translation data; explain precisely how H encodes the
     latter and how the chosen base point normalises it.
  d) Prove all data and homotopies are recovered, establishing isEquiv
     realise-map, not merely surjectivity on classes or existence of a
     compatible H. A chain of mapping-type equivalences may be easier
     than constructing an inverse with two long path calculations.

Steps (b)-(d) are the substantive new theorem. They must be written out
and refereed. Do not label them routine functoriality without a proof.
In particular, do not assume lifts are unique: their ambiguity can
contain [K(A,2),K(B',3)]_*, which does not vanish for general A,B'.
LiftUnique is only usable when its actual set hypothesis holds.

5. IDENTITIES, COMPOSITION AND HOMOTOPY CATEGORIES

Define the intrinsic identity and composition on Mor, using group
homomorphism identities/composition and pasting of pointed squares.

For (a,b,H):t->t' and (a',b',H'):t'->t'', the coefficients are a;a', b;b'.
The homotopy is the composite, with the KFun and associativity bridges:
  K(a;a',2);k''
    = K(a,2);K(a',2);k''
    = K(a,2);k';K(b',4)       (H', precomposed by K(a,2))
    = k;K(b,4);K(b',4)        (H, postcomposed by K(b',4))
    = k;K(b;b',4).
Spell out the paths; coefficient equality alone does not prove equality
of the morphism records. KFun.Comp.k-comp already supplies the K bridges.

Prove realise-map preserves identity and composition as pointed maps.
Then put hom(t,t') = STr(Mor(t,t')). Descend composition, prove category
laws, and obtain bijections to HC(realise t,realise t') compatible with
the existing compC and idC. Combined with object classification, give an
explicit equivalence of homotopy categories (or the object map, inverse,
hom-set bijections and functor/naturality laws if there is no category
library). Do not require the object types to be sets or claim category
univalence from the object classification: these are separate issues.

Avoid constructing a general bicategory or all higher functor coherence.
The full equivalence on mapping types and the homotopy-category laws are
the required deliverables. If operations are initially transported from
pointed maps, still prove agreement with the intrinsic pasting formula.
