  Reviewer's suggested organisation:

      follows from sigma-star and Trunc3.up; compare with the constructed

      substantive step: fibres merely ~ K(B,3) is not enough; the
      identifications must give the family the structure needed for
      classification by K(B,4).  Simple connectivity of the base should
      remove the twisting of B; that argument must be written out.
