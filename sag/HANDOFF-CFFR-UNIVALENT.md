## Plan, in order

1. **Finite free modules and invariance (structure identity).**
   A finite free module of rank n: a module M with ||M ~ R^n|| (linear
   equivalence).  Define without bases: D_k(phi) for phi : M -> N (the ideal
   generated by the coefficients of ∧^k phi against any bases; prove
   independence from the bases by transport, or define it by the
   universal property and compare with `Minors`); exactness; true grade of an
   ideal (already basis-free).  One transport lemma: everything is invariant
   under isomorphism of the data.  Instances to re-derive as tests:
   `koszul/TrueGradeIso` (grade under ring iso), Cor85's subst along
   q + p = N (becomes R^q + R^p ~ R^N), Wiebe's `eop` invariance.
   Main test: **Cor 8.10 in the paper's form** (a, b with a finite free
   resolution of R/(a, b) have a gcd), which removes the FFR encoding
   deviation (Gaps).  Use `koszul/PresentationComparison` if a comparison of
   resolutions is needed.
2. **Canonical determinant line (removes the sign deviation).**  Keep
   ∧^n M as a line, do not identify it with R.  Lemmas 8.2/8.4 with the
   canonical pairing ∧^q ⊗ ∧^p -> ∧^n instead of the Hodge star: the factor
   (-1)^(pq) (Gaps: unstar beta = ±beta*) disappears.  Thm 8.7: the
   resolution F has a canonical line Det(F) (alternating tensor of top
   exterior powers) with a canonical map Det(F) -> R whose image is the gcd
   ideal; independent of the choice of the beta_i (the paper's Cayley
   determinant is only defined up to a unit).
3. **The gcd as a torsor.**  For a regular gcd of (a_i), the type of gcds
   is a merely inhabited R^x-torsor (unique up to a unique unit); the ideal
   (g) is a proposition, hence data by unique choice.  Sharpens Cor 8.10's
   "merely have a gcd" to a canonical object; relate it to the line of 2.
4. **Contractibility of the factorisations.**  Lemma 8.4: for beta fixed
   and regular, the type of gamma with ∧^q B = gamma beta*^T is contractible;
   Cor 8.5: the chain (beta_i, gamma_i) is contractible under the grade
   hypotheses.  Gives Det(F) and the gcd as functions of the resolution,
   hence naturality (base change, isomorphism of resolutions).

Do 1 first (it is the prerequisite of 2 and 3); stop and report after 1 if
the ring-record bridge or levels turn out costly.  Reuse the matrix
statements of `cffr/` as the computational core: the basis-free statements
are proved by choosing a basis (the goal is a proposition, or contractible
data) and applying the cffr declaration.
