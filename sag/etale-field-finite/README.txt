Constructive finiteness: integral descent and Nagata generator reduction
=====================================================================

1. Status and scope

All files in All.seg check. There are 654 new declarations in this directory.
The main construction is NagataReduction: a nonempty sparse polynomial
relation on n+1 generators gives n new generators y and a last element t,
such that the algebra is generated by (y,t), and t satisfies a monic
polynomial over the actual image subalgebra k[y]. No infinitude of k is used.

This is the generator-reduction step, not a new assumed reduction principle.
The input relation, its sparse expansion, its nonempty index, and its root
identity are explicit inputs. Their construction from algebraic dimension
zero and the induction assembly live in the companion developments
etale-field-monomials and etale-field-generated, respectively.
The connected ZeroDimensionalFinite theorem in etale-field-generated now
checks for every finite number of generators, with no Reduction parameter.

The independent one-generator theorem MonogenicFinite.theorem is complete:
a polynomially generated algebra with algebraic dimension zero over a
nonzero discrete field is merely finite as a module. It needs no finite
presentation. It allows dimension-zero witnesses with N=0 and a monic
relation of degree zero (the zero target algebra).

All choices are finite or occur inside propositional truncations. No new
axioms, shared-file edits, or checker changes were made. Nothing is committed.

2. Simplification of the informal proof

Finite presentation and coherent contraction are unnecessary for FINITE SPAN.
Induct on the number of algebra generators. For the last generator x, use
x^N(1-xb)=0 and merely express b=P(x_1,...,x_n). The expression
X_last^N(1-X_last P) is nonzero: its coefficient on the pure last-variable
monomial of degree N is 1. A triangular Nagata change makes this relation
monic in T. The algebra is therefore finite/integral over the image k[y].
Algebraic dimension zero descends through that integral injection, and the
induction hypothesis applies to k[y] without requiring a finite presentation.
Finite spans then compose. The zero-generator case is spanned by 1, even
when the target is the zero algebra.

Finite presentation can still be used separately to obtain a finite matrix
presentation and then an actual basis by Gaussian elimination. This directory
does not claim that a finite spanning family alone provides a basis.

The optional FiniteContraction theorem was proved before this simplification.
It is retained as a reusable lemma but is not used by NagataReduction or the
new finite-span induction route.

The correct iterated identity for integral descent is
  x^N = b^j * x^(j+N).
The proposed identity x^(N+j)=b^j*x^(N+j+1) is false in general and is not used.

3. Exact main interface

NagataReduction.Over[k,setk,dec,nontrivial]
  dec        : for each a, either a=0 or a has an inverse;
  nontrivial : not (1=0) in k.

Its module At[n,S,x,e,sparse,seed,relation] takes:
  S        : a commutative set-valued k-algebra;
  x        : Fin(suc n) -> S;
  e        : Presentations.Expr(Fin(suc n));
  sparse   : a MonomialFamily.SparseData[e] with pairwise distinct exponent
             vectors, nonzero coefficients, and semantic equality to e;
  seed     : Fin sparse.size;
  relation : eval(S,x,e)=0.

It exports:
  y : Fin n -> S, defined by y_i=x_i-t^(axis_i);
  t = x_last;
  B = ExpressionGeneration.Adjoin[S,n,y,t].B, the image k[y] as a subtype;
  generation gen : expression-generation of S by snoc y t;
  polynomial : List B.ring.R;
  degree     : Ring.Nat;
  monic      : coefficient polynomial degree = 1;
  bound      : coefficients at degree+1 and above vanish;
  root       : polynomial evaluated at t via B.incl -> S is zero;
  monic-data : (degree,polynomial,monic,bound,root).

Thus (y,t,generation gen,monic-data) has exactly the structural type required
by GeneratedFiniteInduction.Step in etale-field-generated. The coefficient
algebra B may be the zero ring; no field decision or nontriviality is required
of B. The scaling inverse is selected in the original field k.

The SparseData type is
  MonomialFamily.Over[k,setk,suc n].Normalized[Sparse.zero-dec].SparseData[e]
where Sparse is NagataSparse.Over[k,setk,dec,nontrivial]. Its zero-dec is
constructed from dec and nontrivial. The record definition does not use the
decision parameter, so a record from any definitionally equivalent instance
has the same interface.

4. Construction and reusable interfaces

IntegralDescent:
  ZeroDim[A,setA].Equation x N b = x^N(1-xb)=0.
  ZeroDim.Property = forall x, merely N,b,Equation x N b.
  ZeroDim.from-regular adapts merely x=x*x*b to this property, with N=1.
  Map[A,C,iota].Extension = every element of C is merely the root of a
  monic coefficient-list polynomial with coefficients from A.
  Map.Descent[setA,setC,injective].theorem integral zdC descends Property.
  The proof explicitly clears the monic equation using the valid iterated
  absorption identity, and applies injectivity only at the last step.

FiniteContraction:
  A coherent finite free module and finitely many vectors V_j give finite
  generators for {a | a*e lies in span(V_j)}. The augmented syzygy matrix is
  (V_1,...,V_m,-e), and projection onto the last coordinate gives generators.
  Both membership directions are proved; finite choices are truncated.

FieldLeading and MonogenicFinite:
  Scan a bounded polynomial list for its highest invertible coefficient,
  scale to monic, use MonicQuotientCoordinates for finite spanning powers.
  FromZeroDim constructs the raw relation X^N(1-XP), including N=0.
  The theorem takes polynomial-list generation; MonogenicExpressions in
  etale-field-generated supplies the expression-generation adapter.

NagataWeights:
  Exponent n = Fin n -> Ring.Nat.
  weight M(suc n)u = M*weight M n(old u)+u_last.
  Under the bound u_i<M, this map is injective (explicit radix digit proof).
  Family computes M as one plus the sum of all exponents in the family.
  axis M n has last coordinate 1 and multiplies older axes by M. The axes
  are defined independently of all rings, so the image and target agree.

PolynomialLeading / NagataStretch / NagataMonomials:
  Bounds and top coefficients of raw products; the actual substitution
  T -> T^M as a polynomial universal map; and monic raw lists for the
  transformed monomials. Lists are cut to degree+1 with proved equality.

NagataSemantics:
  The transformed monomial evaluates to the original monomial at
  (y_i+t^(axis_i)). Naturality and coordinate congruence are explicit.

FiniteMaximum / FinitePolynomialSum / NagataFamily:
  Choose an actual maximal index in the nonempty finite family. Distinct
  exponents give distinct weighted degrees. All other rows have zero top
  coefficient, and the sum-delta theorem gives the unique leading scalar.
  Divide by its inverse in k, mapped into the coefficient algebra. The
  full evaluation identity and its root consequence are proved.

NagataSparse / NagataReduction:
  Connect SparseData to the polynomial construction, then construct the
  triangular generators and image algebra. No representatives of all image
  elements are chosen, and no contraction calculation is needed.

6. Limits and integration notes

The all-arity finite-span theorem is assembled in the companion generated
directory. This directory supplies its actual reduction, rather than
postulating it. Its integration must feed in the root relation from the
dimension-zero witness and keep the witness/representative choices inside
the outer truncation. No actual basis is asserted by finite-span results.

The normalized sparse expansion is reused by relative links from
etale-field-monomials. GeneratedSubalgebra, FiniteSpan, and
ExpressionGeneration are reused from etale-field-generated. Other links
point to existing algebra libraries. No imported source was modified.

Some semantic polynomial proofs unfold existing quotient-ring operation and
universal-map aliases, beyond the purely mathematical names in their text.
The largest case opens 28 definitions and takes 0.01 s. The .opens budgets
explicitly include those aliases; no claim of perfect abstraction is made.

Import ordering: FiniteSpan must precede global Spans if that CFN module is
also imported (the language does not allow shadowing). MonicQuotientCoordinates
must precede global Expansion if the etale-local tree is imported. The new
MonogenicFinite uses the local alias PolyExpansion to avoid that collision.

No polynomial ring equivalence or multivariate quotient presentation is
constructed. Only the one-way semantic transformations needed by the proof
are built. The larger CFN import tree is present for the optional contraction
lemma; the Nagata and one-generator proofs themselves do not use it.
