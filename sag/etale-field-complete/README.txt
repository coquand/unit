The complete selected-prime field theorem
========================================

UnramifiedFieldSelected.Over[k,setk,field].At[S,fp,unique,q].theorem

Inputs:
  k       a commutative set-ring;
  field   for each scalar, either equality to zero or an explicit inverse,
          and 1 != 0;
  S       a commutative set-valued k-algebra;
  fp      merely a finite algebra presentation;
  unique  uniqueness of lifting across square-zero ideals;
  q       a specified decidable proper prime, with propositional membership.

Conclusion: merely a Certificate containing a finite dimension, an actual
k-basis of S, and SelectedFactorStatement.Selected for that basis and q.
The latter contains e, z, a monic polynomial P and a derivative inverse w:
  e*e=e and e is outside q;
  e*P(z)=0;
  e*(P'(z)*w-1)=0;
  each basis vector is a polynomial in z after multiplication by e.
SelectedPolynomial extends those representations to arbitrary elements.
Thus S[1/e] is generated by z and has a separable monic annihilator.
An isomorphism S[1/e]=k[T]/(P) is neither asserted nor needed by the local
proof. The discarded factors are not constructed or classified.

GivenBasis[n,basis].theorem gives the exact Selected result for a supplied
basis, while still constructing its perfect trace, reducedness, tensor
diagonal, monic equations and primitive-element certificate. The main
theorem also constructs the basis. finite exports module finiteness.

There is no ZMT hypothesis, field-case principle, primitive-element oracle,
assumed basis, or decision whether the whole field is finite. The prime's
decidability is an intentional hypothesis. No prime existence is asserted.

Construction

1. UnramifiedFieldStructure constructs finite span, finite basis and trace
   duality from fp and Unique. UnramifiedFieldRegular supplies a=a^2*b,
   hence square-zero elements vanish. DiagonalCertificate constructs the
   actual tensor coproduct and averaging idempotent, under truncation.
2. FiniteMonicCoefficientFamily constructs monic annihilators of the basis
   vectors and records a finite family containing every lower coefficient.
   FiniteCoefficientField either constructs n^3+1 distinct scalars or an
   actual finite coefficient subfield containing that family.
3. The large branch constructs z with Delta(z)*w=1-epsilon. The content
   of epsilon+sum Delta(b_i) T^i is unimodular. Polynomial McCoy, matrix
   McCoy, an actual k[T]-basis, a determinant bound, the root bound and
   adjugate specialization produce the inverse. Tensor contraction then
   gives the coordinate identities for generation on a selected factor.
4. The small branch lifts the constructed monic equations to the finite
   subfield, constructs the finite image subalgebra, selects its field
   factor and constructs a primitive element by finite multiplicative-
   order search. CornerHorner preserves the idempotent in value and
   derivative identities under the nonunital inclusion into S.
5. Both branches return the exact same Selected certificate. Only finite
   choices are made, within truncation. All eliminations have propositional
   targets; no global selection of representatives is used.

The branch assembly lives in etale-field-selected-complete; this directory
removes its explicit basis, trace, reducedness and diagonal inputs. All
reused proof files are relative links. Nothing in those developments or
the checker was edited by this wrapper.

FieldSelectedRegression applies the final theorem to F2 and the actual
standard algebra for X^2-X at its point 1. Both start with a finite
presentation and Unique, with no supplied span, basis, trace, diagonal
or selected factor. The selected prime on the quadratic is constructed
as the inverse image of zero at the point. The polynomial presentation
representatives themselves are obtained under truncation. A second base
test checks the GivenBasis interface. Imported milestone regressions
exercise both coefficient-closure outcomes, nontrivial tensor complements,
small fields, characteristic 2, empty families and zero ranks; their
README files record independent mutation checks.

Remaining local-base integration

This proves the requested selected field case, not the final unconditional
flat+unramified=>etale theorem over arbitrary local bases. The checked
selected-factor lifting bridge must receive the actual localized special
fibre with its FP/Unique data, compatible specified prime, quasi-inverse
relation, and local ZMT data. Equalities pulled back through B/mB -> C
retain explicit powers of s; that map is never assumed injective.
etale-field-local-complete now connects this theorem to that bridge,
removing its supplied basis and selected-certificate hypotheses. The
remaining local inputs just listed are still explicit.

No new axioms. All.seg has only the existing univalence, replacement and
surjection parameters. No checker or shared-source edits; no commits.
