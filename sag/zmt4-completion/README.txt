ZMT4: COMPLETE for at most three affine generators
=================================================

ENTRY POINT: FinalZMT4.seg, imported by All.seg.

FinalZMT4.universal3 PROVES CFNDescent.UniversalZMT 3. It is not a
parameter. FinalZMT4.universal2/universal1/universal0 prove the smaller

FinalZMT4.Theorem[R,setR,I,propI,Ea,arity,x,gen,fo].result
is the usual arbitrary-base finite-localization theorem for arity
  generators0, generators1, generators2, generators3.
The corresponding number of affine algebra generators is 0,1,2,3.

HYPOTHESES:
 * R any commutative set-ring; no CFN/domain/field/characteristic hypothesis;
 * I an ideal predicate on R with propositional membership;
 * E an R-algebra generated by the specified finite family x;
 * E/IE merely finite as a module over R/I, via QuotientFibre's quotient API.
CONCLUSION, merely:
 * s in E with s-1 in IE;
 * the actual image subalgebra B=R[s,sx_1,...,sx_n] inside E is finite over R;
 * inclusion induces a ring map B[1/s]->E[1/s] that is an equivalence.
The construction is the checked FiniteFibreLocalisation conclusion,
now instantiated with PROVED UniversalZMT principles.

Scope: P^3 / at most3 affine generators. This does not prove the
arbitrary-dimensional ZMT, generic weighted/Rees theorem, or the later
local etale structure theorem. The earlier zmt4/ README correctly records
where that earlier milestone stopped; this NEW directory completes it.

All source additions are in new directories, connected by relative links.
No earlier/shared source or checker was modified, no new axioms or Theory

1. Proof chain
---------------
FiniteFibreSections4 constructs explicit sections from the residual monic
equations. It uses monic-friendly homogeneous generators P with deh(P)=0;
it does not need equality with the earlier homogenisation ZMT4Setup.P.
Their degrees and dehomogenisation are proved in MonicFriendlySetup.

The section formula is simpler than the original chart-idempotent plan:
for H^p=1+uQp, choose common M>=delta*p and M>=delta, L=M+1, and put
  a=sum_(i<L)(-uQp)^i, b=(-Qp)^L, t=b*u^M, z=t/X_k.
Then 1-u*t=a*H^p exactly. Overlaps use
  z-w=z*(1-Z*w)-w*(1-Z*z),
with explicit powers of the residual relations to witness the errors.
No separate idempotence calculation is needed. Native Laurent chart rings,
coordinate inverses, restrictions, degree projections, and finite-support
assembly are all constructed, including the chart Z normalisation Z^-1.

IdealPowerReesLift4 lifts the explicit ideal-power trees to actual Rees
cochains c_n, homogeneous in U of degree n+1. This is an explicit function
of n and its witness, not a sequence of mere choices.

ReesExchange4 exchanges S[U] and A[U][X] by inverse ring maps, identifies
the old canonical biT lists with their actual elements, preserves
coefficient homogeneity and commutes with evaluation.
ReesPolynomialMembership4 applies the constructed elimination kernel list.
The linked zmt4-rees-lifts development clears denominators on every chart
intersection, projects the coefficients to the required X-degrees, and
assembles actual KG lifts. Nothing about flatness or tensor powers is used.

ReesDifferentials4 proves evaluation commutes with delta and J-images are
stable under delta. Together with delta^2=0 this proves the lifts satisfy
exactly the unchanged finite correction theorem's hypotheses.
ZMT4FromSections constructs those inputs, applies correction, extracts the
integral certificate, and eliminates the finite-list truncation only into
the truncated conclusion. No infinite choice is needed.

ZMT4ExtractionGeneral is the previous extraction proof parameterized by
homogeneous generators and deh(P)=0; its global support/exponent lemmas
and the previous degree-lowering/determinant certificate theorem are reused.
The residual certificate is instantiated at Z[C_1,...,C_m], giving
FinalZMT4.universal3. ResidualPadding adjoins a zero generator, transports
certificates back, and gives the smaller arities including zero.
The already checked arbitrary-base descent and finite-localization theorem
then supply FinalZMT4.Theorem, with no unproved principle left as an input.
