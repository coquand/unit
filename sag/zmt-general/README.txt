Algebraic ZMT in arbitrary finite dimension: COMPLETE
====================================================

1. Public theorem

FinalZMTGeneral.Theorem[R,setR,I,propI,Ea,n,x,gen,fo].result
has the following hypotheses, with n an unrestricted natural number:

 - R is a commutative set-ring.
 - I is an ideal of R with propositional membership.
 - Ea is an R-algebra E, generated by the given finite family x:Fin n->E.
   Generation is stated as mere existence of a polynomial expression for
   each e in E; no global expression choice is supplied.
 - E/IE is merely finite as a module over R/I, using the existing
   QuotientFibre quotient-ring construction.

Conclusion (Theorem.Conclusion), merely:
 there is s in E such that
   s-1 belongs to IE;
   B=R[s,sx_1,...,sx_n] is finite as an R-module and contains s;
   the canonical ring map B[1/s] -> E[1/s] is an equivalence.

B is the actual image subalgebra, with carrier a subtype of E. Its
finiteness includes a finite spanning family. ForS[s].LI.phi is the
canonical ring map, and the conclusion includes
   Prelude.isEquiv LI.Bs.R LI.Es.R LI.phi.1,
so it retains which ring map is an isomorphism. It does not just assert
an unspecified equivalence of the two carriers.

There is NO field, domain, Noetherian, CFN or dimension restriction on R.
There is NO UniversalZMT, CFNZMT, section, correction, resolution,
finite stable module or integrality principle among the theorem inputs.
The existing ambient univalence, replacement-images and surjection
parameters of All.Theory are unchanged. No new axiom is introduced.

2. The previously conditional principle is now proved

 FinalZMTGeneral.universal : (n:Nat) -> CFNDescent.UniversalZMT n

For dimensions m=extra+2, FinalZMTGeneral.Residual constructs the
certificate over ANY CFN base from residual data alone. Instantiation at
the proved CFN coefficient ring Z[C_1,...,C_q] gives large extra.
UniversalArityGlue identifies extra+2 with suc(suc extra), and uses the
proved ResidualPadding.lower twice for dimensions1 and0.
Thus all finite dimensions are covered, including the empty generator
family. There is no bound by P^3 or by three affine generators.
The internal projective chart construction for affine dimension m uses
m+1 homogeneous coordinates, with the newest coordinate Z set to1.

The final theorem then reuses the checked arbitrary-base descent,
quotient-fibre bridge, image-subalgebra finiteness and localisation
construction from zmt-descent. The stronger public localisation result
is assembled inside the certificate truncation; older files are unchanged.

3. Proof and directory structure

 zmt-certificate-general:
   Generic ordinary dependence in n=extra+3 homogeneous coordinates,
   constructed finite syzygy stages, chart exactness and finite zigzag.
 zmt-weighted-general:
   Coefficient grading and weighted dependence, evaluation with explicit
   ideal-power witnesses. No flatness or tensor-image identification.
 zmt-laurent-general, zmt-assembly-general, zmt-sections-general:
   Native chart localisations and restrictions; residual monic equations
   give explicit finite-fibre sections with controlled differential and
   compatibility with the initial Z^{-1} section.
 zmt-rees-general, zmt-correction-general:
   Constructed finite bihomogeneous Rees kernel list, variable-order
   exchange, localized lifts, factorisation through the original equations,
   and a corrected section closed modulo the original ideal J.
 zmt-extract-exponents-general, zmt-extract-general:
   Common finite support bounds clear singleton and edge denominators.
   Coefficient extensionality makes genuine homogeneous polynomial
   identities. Evaluation kills J, yielding s=1 modulo (b) and relations
     s*y_i^(a_i+1)=deh(q_i), degree(q_i)=a_i.
 zmt-certificate-extract-general:
   From these relations construct ONE finite module spanned by monomials
   of total degree <= D+sum_i a_i, containing1 and stable under s and all
   s*y_i. The determinant trick yields the required monic equations.
 zmt-general:
   Evaluation/coordinate helpers, finite-support extraction wrapper,
   final all-arity construction and the arbitrary-base theorem above.

All supports, representatives, finite lists and certificates are used
under propositional truncation. Choices occur only over finite families;
no countable/global choice of section supports is made.
The fixed-coordinate ZMT4 proof is not used to prove the large-dimensional
principle. Its dimension-independent scalar algebra and padding helpers
are reused by relative links.

regressions include a nonreduced four-generator example with nonzero
