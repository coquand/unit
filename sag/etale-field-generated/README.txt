Generated subalgebras and the finite-span induction
=================================================

Status: the full arbitrary-arity finite-span theorem checks. The monic
coordinate reduction is constructed, not assumed. Nothing is committed; no
shared file, checker or axiom was changed.

Interfaces

GeneratedSubalgebra.Over[R,setR].At[S,n,x] constructs R[x] as a subtype
of S. Membership merely supplies an expression representative. It exports
algebra, inclusion, incl-inj, variables, and generated. Its Generators
predicate is the usual expression-generation predicate on S.

FiniteSpan.Over[R,C,h]:
  Coordinates n v c = (lambda:Fin n -> R) *
                     (sum_i h(lambda_i)*v_i = c).
  Data = (n:Nat) * (v:Fin n -> C) * (forall c, ||Coordinates n v c||).
  Finite = ||Data||.
Retarget transfers data across pointwise equal structure maps.

FiniteRectangle flattens a rectangular family and proves its paired sum
formula. FiniteSpanComposition composes finite spans using the fixed
products of their generators, with finite choice only inside truncation.

OneGeneratorPolynomial converts expression representatives into raw
polynomial lists over any set-ring. MonicGeneratedFinite then proves
finiteness from a monic relation over any set-ring, with degree zero
allowed. MonogenicExpressions applies the field-specific nonzero-relation
construction in etale-field-finite to the same generation interface.

ExpressionGeneration.Change transports generation under polynomial
changes of generators. NoGenerators gives the finite span by 1 without
deciding whether the target is the zero ring. Adjoin proves that an
algebra generated by (y,t) is monogenic over its generated image R[y].

FiniteSpanIntegral applies DeterminantTrick to a finite span, choosing
coordinates only for 1 and the finite family x*v_i. It exports precisely
IntegralDescent.Map.Extension: every element merely satisfies a monic
coefficient-list equation. MonicLists/MonicWitness bridge the actual
characteristic polynomial to this list, without assuming a representation.

GeneratedFiniteInduction proves the induction step and assembles it for
every number of generators. Its explicit Reduction input requires:
given a generated algebra on n+1 variables, and zero-dimensionality,
merely new generators (y_1,...,y_n,t) and a monic equation for t over
the image R[y]. From this it constructs the intermediate image, its
integrality and zero-dimensionality, and composes the finite spans.
This reusable induction theorem keeps Reduction as an explicit parameter.

ZeroDimensionalRelation constructs a rooted nonempty sparse relation from
zero-dimensionality and expression generation. If t is the last generator,
choose t^N(1-t*b)=0 and an expression P for b. MonomialDifference constructs
T^N-T^(N+1)P with a distinguished coefficient 1 and distinct exponents.
Its sparse data records evaluation in every algebra and thus lives in
U l+. All choices stay under truncation, including the change of universe.

ZeroDimensionalFinite.Over[k,setk,dec,nontrivial].theorem n S x gen zd
  : FiniteSpan.Over[k,S.ring,S.iota].Finite

Here dec is zero-or-unit, nontrivial is 1 != 0, gen is expression
generation by x:Fin n -> S, and zd is the algebraic dimension-zero
property forall t, merely N,b with t^N(1-t*b)=0. NagataReduction turns
the constructed sparse relation into the exact Reduction input; then
GeneratedFiniteInduction proves the result for all n, including n=0.
No finite presentation, basis, prime-ideal interpretation, or ZMT is
assumed. The image subalgebras need not be finitely presented.
