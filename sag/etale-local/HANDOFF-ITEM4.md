g = G(y) is a unit).  The integrality hypotheses of Section1 only use the
equations `y b_i = sum_j b_j a_ji` and `1 = sum c_k b_k` for the images of

  iota(mu_j) t_j`, mu_j in m, t_j in T.  Use LISTS of pairs (mu, t) (or

  J-zero (empty), J-m (one pair (a, 1)).

Step.  Write t_j = r_j(y) g^-(k_j) (fullness) and K = sum_j k_j.  Then
  g^K H(y) = sum_j iota(mu_j) r_j(y) g^(K - k_j)

(`LoopInputs.product` gives Horner(G^e r) = G(y)^e r(y)).  Avoid
subtraction of exponents: K - k_j is the sum of the other k_i; or clear
denominators one pair at a time by induction on the list (multiply the
running identity by g^(k_j)), which is simpler.  This is `cert` with
W = G^K H, i.e. n := K (the note's n = m + l; here no l is needed because
the equality is used in E_g, not in R[y]; say so in the file comment).
