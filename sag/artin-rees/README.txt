Definitions (Coherent.seg, CFN.seg)
-----------------------------------

For a module Md over A (IdealPowers.Mod):
  Disc   (D) membership in the span of a finite family is decidable; the
         decision is data, the witness is truncated.
  Coh    (C) the syzygies of a finite family are generated by a finite
         family of syzygies (truncated).
  FL     the principle of free lists, for proposition-valued predicates.
  CFN K0 A  (D), (C) for A as a module over itself, and (N): merely a
         presentation of A whose codes are El K0 c for some c : CT
         (data CT = base | list c; El K0 base = K0, El K0 (list c) =
         List (El K0 c)), noetherian in the sense of Coquand-Persson,
         surjective under truncation (CodedNoeth.NP).  The base K0 is a
         parameter (k.R for a discrete field; the integer codes for §7).
         A proposition in U l (CFN.Noeth has the shape of
         Noetheriannote.Calculus.Noeth; U l : U l is rejected, checked).
         FL follows (CFN.fl).
The decision of membership (D) is an explicit, named hypothesis (Ruitenburg's
"with detachable ideals"); see artin-rees.tex, Remark after Def. 1.
Why codes: A[X] is a quotient of lists; a bar on its elements would need
choice, and with a truncated decision a canonical tree only bars a Good
stated with mere membership, which Hilbert.seg cannot take as input.
